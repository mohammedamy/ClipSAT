The Free-Tier Promise

Written before any paywall exists, so that when one does, it's built against a public commitment — not an evolving internal guess.

Draft v1 · Last updated 2026-08-08 · Any change to this page is dated below, never silent

The commitment

Everything that is free on ClipSAT today stays free forever. A future paid tier will sell convenience, coaching, and institutional tooling on top — it will never move a proof, an explorer, or a chapter that is free right now behind a paywall.

Status

ClipSAT has no accounts, payments, or paid tier today — everything on the site is free. This page exists so that changes, whenever they come, get judged against a written promise instead of drifting. Treat this draft as pending Mr. Abdallah's sign-off before it's presented as final.

The vow

If a paywall is ever built, whatever is free the day before it ships stays free after it ships. This page will be updated with a dated note describing the change, not silently edited.

ClipSAT Plus is priced at $20/year, after a 30-day free trial — anchored to be affordable for a self-motivated individual student first, reach over margin, especially early on. That price applies to Plus only; it is never a cost of the free floor above.

A lapsed or unpurchased Plus trial never locks you out of the free floor. It ends the trial's extra features and reverts your account to everything in the free-forever list — never to a blocked or paywalled site.

If this promise is ever broken, that's a bug in ClipSAT's word, not a pricing decision — and it should be reported the same way any other bug would be.
