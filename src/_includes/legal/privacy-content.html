Privacy Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

ClipSAT is a free, comprehensive mathematics study platform by Mr. Mohamed Abdallah, serving students preparing for Saudi national examinations (GAT Qudrat, SAAT Tahsili) and international curricula (AP, Cambridge IGCSE/A-Level, IB, SAT, ACT, EST, and university mathematics). We believe in privacy by design, minimal data collection, and full transparency. This policy explains what data exists, how it is processed, and your rights, in compliance with applicable standards including the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

1. What's stored locally on your device

  • Study Progress & Preferences: Your completed chapters, accuracy stats, streak counts, mistake log, flashcard review schedule, and dark-mode setting are stored directly in your browser's local storage (localStorage). This data remains on your device by default and is never transmitted to ClipSAT without your explicit action.
  • Clearing Data: You can reset your local study data at any time from within the platform (via the Progress or Mistake reset buttons) or by clearing your browser's site data.

2. Cloud Sync & User Accounts (Optional)

To enable seamless cross-device synchronization between your phone, tablet, and computer, ClipSAT offers an optional free account system backed by Supabase:

  • Passwordless Authentication: Sign-in uses magic links or one-time verification codes sent to your email address. We do not store passwords.
  • Sync Scope: Only your study progress, mistake items, and chapter completion marks are synchronized to your private account record, protected by database Row Level Security (RLS) so that only you can access your records.
  • Account Deletion: You can delete your cloud account and all associated synchronized records at any time by requesting deletion via our support channels.

3. AI Tutor ("Ask Mr. Mohamed") & Practice Generation

When you interact with the AI Tutor or generate AI practice questions:

  • Server-Side Proxy: All requests route through a secure Supabase Edge Function. API keys (Groq / OpenAI) are stored strictly as server secrets and are never exposed in browser bundles, client code, or user storage.
  • Data Protection & Rate Limits: Requests are gated behind authenticated user sessions, with server-side per-user daily quotas, per-IP rate limiting, character length limits, and automated content safety filters to prevent abuse and protect student safety.
  • Purpose Limitation: Prompts and math questions are processed solely to return pedagogical explanations and generated practice questions. Your prompts are not used to train public foundation models without consent.

4. Advertising, Analytics & Cookie Consent

ClipSAT uses essential browser storage to save your learning progress. With your permission via our cookie consent banner, third-party advertising cookies (such as Google Ads) or analytics scripts may be loaded to support and keep the platform 100% free for students worldwide.

  • Opt-In Consent: Non-essential advertising and tracking scripts are blocked until you click "Accept All" on the cookie consent banner. If you choose "Essential Only", only necessary local storage is utilized.
  • Changing Consent: You can change or revoke your cookie choices at any time by clicking in the site footer.
  • You may also manage personalized advertising preferences directly at Google Ads Settings.

5. Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) Compliance

For users in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, ClipSAT complies with the principles of the Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree No. M/19 and amended by Royal Decree No. M/148):

  • Fair and Lawful Processing: Personal data is processed fairly, lawfully, and transparently based on user consent.
  • Purpose Limitation: We collect and process data solely for providing educational content, tracking study progress, and powering interactive learning tools.
  • Data Minimization: We restrict collection to the minimum data strictly required to deliver our educational services.
  • User Rights: Under the PDPL, you have the right to be informed about the data collected, access your data, request corrections, destroy or erase your data, and withdraw consent at any time.

6. Visitor Counter & Cookieless Analytics

The home page displays an anonymous, live visit count stored in our Supabase database. Each browser tab increments the shared counter at most once per session using session storage. ClipSAT also uses privacy-first, cookieless analytics to record aggregate educational interactions (such as diagnostic test completions and feature usage). No personal data, tracking cookies, device fingerprints, or cross-site browsing histories are collected or transmitted.

7. Google Forms Integration

Teachers can optionally connect a Google account to export ClipSAT quizzes and worksheets into Google Forms for use in Google Classroom or other learning management systems. This integration is entirely opt-in:

  • Requests are scoped strictly to creating Forms and uploading question diagrams to the teacher's own Google Drive. ClipSAT never reads or accesses personal files, emails, or student rosters.
  • Tokens are handled securely and access can be revoked at any time via your Google Account permissions.
