Cookie Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

ClipSAT is committed to privacy and data transparency. This policy explains what browser storage mechanisms and cookies are used on ClipSAT, why they are used, and how you can manage your preferences at any time, in alignment with our Privacy Policy.

1. Essential Local & Session Storage

By default, ClipSAT relies on browser local storage and session storage rather than third-party tracking cookies:

  • Local Storage: Your chapter progress, study streaks, accuracy metrics, mistake log, flashcard review intervals, and dark-mode appearance preference are stored locally on your device. If you use cloud accounts, your secure authentication session token is also retained locally so you do not need to sign in on every visit. This data is never sold to third parties and can be cleared through your browser settings.
  • Session Storage: The home page visitor counter uses a temporary session key to ensure page navigation within a single tab does not inflate the public visitor tally. This value clears automatically when you close your browser tab.

2. Cookie Consent & Third-Party Cookies

To help maintain free access to our mathematical resources, ClipSAT may serve contextual or relevant advertisements via Google Ads or measure aggregate usage via privacy-focused analytics:

  • Essential Only: If you decline non-essential cookies on our consent banner, no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are set. Only necessary storage for your learning tools remains active.
  • Accept All: If you accept cookies, Google and third-party advertising partners may use cookies to serve personalized or contextual advertisements based on visits to this and other websites.
  • Control & Withdraw Consent: You can change or revoke your consent anytime by clicking in the website footer.

3. Contact & Inquiries

Questions regarding our cookie practices or browser storage can be sent directly to support@clipsat.org, or via WhatsApp (+966 59 768 8647) or Telegram (@ClipSAT22).
