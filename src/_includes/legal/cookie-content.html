Cookie Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

ClipSAT is committed to privacy and data transparency. This policy explains what browser storage and cookies are used on ClipSAT, why they are used, and how you can manage your preferences at any time, in alignment with the Privacy Policy.

1. Essential Local Storage (No Cookies)

By default, ClipSAT relies on browser local storage rather than cookies. Your chapter progress, streaks, accuracy statistics, mistake log, flashcard reviews, and dark-mode theme preference stay stored safely on your device. This data is never sent to third parties and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

2. Cookie Consent & Third-Party Cookies

To help fund and maintain free access to ClipSAT, the site may serve advertisements via Google Ads or measure traffic via privacy-respecting analytics:

  • Essential Only: If you decline non-essential cookies, no advertising or tracking cookies are set. Only necessary local storage for your learning progress is active.
  • Accept All: If you accept cookies, Google and third-party advertising partners may use cookies to serve personalized or contextual advertisements based on visits to this and other websites.
  • Control & Withdraw Consent: You can change your choice anytime by clicking in our website footer.

3. Anonymous Visitor Counter

The home page displays an anonymous, live visit count powered by ClipSAT's Supabase backend. Each browser tab registers one count per visit using session storage (which automatically clears when you close the tab) so that page navigation does not inflate the count. No tracking cookies or personal identifiers are stored.
