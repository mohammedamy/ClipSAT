Cookie Policy

Last updated July 13, 2026

ClipSAT does not set any cookies itself, and runs no advertising or analytics scripts. The one exception is a small visitor-count image on the home page, described below. This page explains exactly what's stored, using the same plain terms as the Privacy Policy.

What ClipSAT itself stores

Your progress, streaks, mistake log, flashcard review schedule, and dark-mode preference are saved using your browser's local storage — a different, more limited mechanism than cookies. This data stays on your device, is never transmitted to any ClipSAT server, and isn't shared with anyone. You can clear it at any time from your browser's settings.

Visitor counter

The home page shows a live count as a small image loaded from a third-party badge service, hits.sh. It counts each time that image loads, not verified-unique visitors, so it's an approximate tally. Loading that image sends your device's IP address and basic browser information to hits.sh, the same way any embedded image works — the badge itself does not set cookies or use local storage, and ClipSAT never receives or stores that information. If you'd rather not load it, blocking image requests to hits.sh in your browser or an ad blocker won't affect anything else on the site.

Third-party embeds

Links to WhatsApp, Telegram, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and PayPal take you to those companies' own sites, each of which may set its own cookies under its own policy once you're there. ClipSAT doesn't control or receive data from those cookies.

If this changes

If ClipSAT adds advertising or analytics in the future, this page will be updated first to describe exactly what's added and why, along with a way to control it, before anything goes live.
