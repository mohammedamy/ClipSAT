A poll of 800 voters has a margin of error of ±3.5%\pm 3.5\%±3.5%. Which of the following would produce a margin of error of approximately ±1.75%\pm 1.75\%±1.75%?
A. Surveying 1,600 voters
B. Surveying 3,200 voters
C. Surveying 6,400 voters
D. Surveying 2,400 voters
Correct Answer: (B) — Surveying 3,200 voters
Halving the margin of error (from 3.5% to 1.75%) requires multiplying the sample size by 22=42^2 = 422=4: 800×4=3,200800 \times 4 = 3,200800×4=3,200 voters.
