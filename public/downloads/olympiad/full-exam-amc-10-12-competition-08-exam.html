D. 111
Correct Answer: (A) — 12\frac{1}{2}21​
The equation of a unit circle centered at the origin is x2+y2=1x^2 + y^2 = 1x2+y2=1. We are given that the y-coordinate of point PPP is y=32y = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}y=23​​. Substituting this into the equation of the unit circle:
x2+(32)2=1x^2 + (\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2})^2 = 1x2+(23​​)2=1
x2+34=1x^2 + \frac{3}{4} = 1x2+43​=1
x2=1−34x^2 = 1 - \frac{3}{4}x2=1−43​

The information about the y-coordinate serves as a consistency check and confirms that the point is indeed on the unit circle in the first quadrant, as sin⁡θ=y=32\sin \theta = y = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}sinθ=y=23​​ and (12)2+(32)2=14+34=1(\frac{1}{2})^2 + (\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2})^2 = \frac{1}{4} + \frac{3}{4} = 1(21​)2+(23​​)2=41​+43​=1.
