In normal-play Nim with three heaps of sizes (5,  8,  11)(5,\; 8,\; 11)(5,8,11) (illustrated), a winning move exists on the first heap (size 555). How many stones must the first player remove from that heap of size 555 to leave a balanced Nim-sum 000 position?
A. 222 ✓
B. 333
C. 666
D. −2-2−2
Correct Answer: (A) — 222
Compute the binary Nim-sum X=5⊕8⊕11=6X = 5 \oplus 8 \oplus 11 = 6X=5⊕8⊕11=6. To restore Nim-sum 000 by playing on the heap of size 555, its new size must be 5⊕6=3<55 \oplus 6 = 3 < 55⊕6=3<5. Therefore, the player must remove 5−3=25 - 3 = 25−3=2 stones.
