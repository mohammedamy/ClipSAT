15 Trigonometry Standard [5 marks]

Official Examiner Mark Scheme [5 marks] — Final Answer: 12.5 cm
Mark Scheme (CAIE / Edexcel Grading Steps):
- Step 1 (Method & Formulation): Identify the required geometric/algebraic relationship from the diagram and substitute the given values. [M1]
- Step 2 (Derivation & Calculation): We are given a right-angled triangle ABC, where angle ABC = 90°. We know that AB = 10 cm and angle BAC = θ\thetaθ. We are also given that sin⁡θ=35\sin \theta = \frac{3}{5}sinθ=53​.

In a right-angled triangle, the sine of an angle is defined as the ratio of the length of the side opposite the angle to the length of the hypotenuse. Therefore, sin⁡θ=OppositeHypotenuse\sin \theta = \frac{\text{Opposite}}{\text{Hypotenuse}}sinθ=HypotenuseOpposite​.

In triangle ABC, the side opposite to angle θ\thetaθ (angle BAC) is BC, and the hypotenuse is AC.

So, sin⁡θ=BCAC\sin \theta = \frac{BC}{AC}sinθ=ACBC​.

We are given sin⁡θ=35\sin \theta = \frac{3}{5}sinθ=53​, so BCAC=35\frac{BC}{AC} = \frac{3}{5}ACBC​=53​. This means that BC = 3k and AC = 5k for some constant k.

We are given the length of AB = 10 cm. We can use the Pythagorean theorem in triangle ABC: AB2+BC2=AC2AB^2 + BC^2 = AC^2AB2+BC2=AC2.

Substituting the values:
102+(3k)2=(5k)210^2 + (3k)^2 = (5k)^2102+(3k)2=(5k)2
100+9k2=25k2100 + 9k^2 = 25k^2100+9k2=25k2
100=25k2−9k2100 = 25k^2 - 9k^2100=25k2−9k2
100=16k2100 = 16k^2100=16k2
k2=10016k^2 = \frac{100}{16}k2=16100​
k=10016k = \sqrt{\frac{100}{16}}k=16100​​ (since length must be positive)
k=104k = \frac{10}{4}k=410​
k=2.5k = 2.5k=2.5

Now we can find the length of AC, which is our hypotenuse:
AC = 5k
AC = 5 * 2.5
AC = 12.5 cm.

Therefore, the length of AC is 12.5 cm.

Common algebraic traps include incorrectly applying the Pythagorean theorem or misinterpreting the sine ratio. [M1]
