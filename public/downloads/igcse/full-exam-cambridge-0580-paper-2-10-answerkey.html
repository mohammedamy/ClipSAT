15 Probability & Statistics Standard [5 marks]
The histogram shows the distribution of scores for a group of students in a mathematics test. The total number of students who scored between 0 and 40 is 32. The frequency density for the class interval 20 ≤x<40\leq x < 40≤x<40 is 0.80.80.8. Calculate the number of students who scored between 20 and 40. [5]
Official Examiner Mark Scheme [5 marks] — Final Answer: 161616
Mark Scheme (CAIE / Edexcel Grading Steps):
- Step 1 (Method & Formulation): Identify the required geometric/algebraic relationship from the diagram and substitute the given values. [M1]
- Step 2 (Derivation & Calculation): The area of a bar in a histogram represents the frequency of the corresponding class interval. The area is calculated by frequency density multiplied by the class width.

The class interval 20 ≤x<40\leq x < 40≤x<40 has a width of 40−20=2040 - 20 = 2040−20=20.
The frequency density for this interval is given as 0.80.80.8.

Therefore, the frequency (number of students) in this interval is calculated as:
Frequency = Frequency Density ×\times× Class Width
Frequency = 0.8×200.8 \times 200.8×20
Frequency = 16

Thus, 16 students scored between 20 and 40. [M1]
- Step 3 (Final Accuracy): Verified exact result: 16. [A1]
