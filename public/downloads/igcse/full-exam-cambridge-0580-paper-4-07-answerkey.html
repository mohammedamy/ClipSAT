Formula: n(F∪G)=n(F)+n(G)−n(F∩G)=32+25−14=43n(F \cup G) = n(F) + n(G) - n(F \cap G) = 32 + 25 - 14 = 43n(F∪G)=n(F)+n(G)−n(F∩G)=32+25−14=43. Neither = 50−43=750 - 43 = 750−43=7. [M1 A1] [M1][A1]
