6 Geometry Foundational [4 marks]
In the diagram, ABC is a triangle with angle ABC = 90 degrees. AD is the altitude to the hypotenuse BC. Given that BD = 4 cm and DC = 9 cm, find the length of the altitude AD. [4]
Official Examiner Mark Scheme [4 marks] — Final Answer: 6 cm
Mark Scheme (CAIE / Edexcel Grading Steps):
- Step 1 (Method & Formulation): Identify the required geometric/algebraic relationship from the diagram and substitute the given values. [M1]
- Step 2 (Derivation & Calculation): In a right-angled triangle, the altitude drawn to the hypotenuse forms two smaller triangles that are similar to the original triangle and to each other. Therefore, triangle ABD is similar to triangle CAD.

From the similarity of these triangles, we have the proportion of corresponding sides:
ADBD=DCAD\frac{AD}{BD} = \frac{DC}{AD}BDAD​=ADDC​

Let AD = h. Substituting the given values:
h4=9h\frac{h}{4} = \frac{9}{h}4h​=h9​

Cross-multiplying gives:
h2=4×9h^2 = 4 \times 9h2=4×9
h2=36h^2 = 36h2=36

Taking the square root of both sides:
h=36h = \sqrt{36}h=36​
h=6h = 6h=6

Thus, the length of the altitude AD is 6 cm. [M1]
- Step 3 (Final Accuracy): Verified exact result: 6 cm. [A1]
