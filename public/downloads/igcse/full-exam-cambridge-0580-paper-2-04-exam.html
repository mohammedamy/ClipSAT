- Step 2 (Derivation & Calculation): Let the original triangle ABC have vertices A(-2, 2), B(1, 3), and C(-1, 1).

Step 1: Reflection in the line y=xy = xy=x.
When a point (x,y)(x, y)(x,y) is reflected in the line y=xy = xy=x, its image is (y,x)(y, x)(y,x).
Applying this to triangle ABC:
A(-2, 2) becomes A'(2, -2).
B(1, 3) becomes B'(3, 1).
C(-1, 1) becomes C'(1, -1).

Step 2: Translation of triangle A'B'C'.
Triangle A'B'C' is translated by the vector (−31)\begin{pmatrix} -3 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}(−31​). This means we subtract 3 from the x-coordinate and add 1 to the y-coordinate of each vertex.
Applying this to triangle A'B'C':
A'(2, -2) becomes A''(2 - 3, -2 + 1) = A''(-1, -1).
B'(3, 1) becomes B''(3 - 3, 1 + 1) = B''(0, 2).
C'(1, -1) becomes C''(1 - 3, -1 + 1) = C''(-2, 0).
