- Step 2 (Derivation & Calculation): The transformation from point A to its image A' can be represented by a translation vector. The change in the x-coordinate is the difference between the x-coordinate of A' and the x-coordinate of A. The change in the y-coordinate is the difference between the y-coordinate of A' and the y-coordinate of A.
