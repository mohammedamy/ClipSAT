A marketing research firm is analyzing the relationship between the preferred social media platform and age group of consumers. They collected data from a sample and summarized it in the two-way table below. The marginal distributions are also provided. The research team is interested in understanding how the distribution of preferred platform varies across age groups and is considering a chi-square test for independence. If the null hypothesis of independence is rejected, the observed counts will be significantly different from what would be expected under independence.

Assume that a chi-square test for independence is to be performed on this data. Which of the following represents a potential challenge or insight derived from the structure of the data and the test's assumptions?
A. The number of degrees of freedom for the chi-square test can be determined solely from the total number of observations, irrespective of the table dimensions.
B. The calculation of expected counts under the null hypothesis of independence relies on the marginal proportions of the observed data. ✓
C. If the expected count in any cell is less than 5, a normal approximation to the binomial distribution for each cell count would be appropriate to proceed with the chi-square test.
D. A high chi-square statistic indicates that the observed counts are very close to the expected counts, suggesting a strong association between age group and preferred social media platform.
Correct Answer: (B) — The calculation of expected counts under the null hypothesis of independence relies on the marginal proportions of the observed data.
The chi-square test for independence assumes that the expected counts in each cell are sufficiently large, typically at least 5. Option A is incorrect because degrees of freedom depend on the number of rows and columns: df = (r-1)(c-1). Option B is incorrect; if expected counts are too small, alternative methods like Fisher's exact test or combining categories might be necessary, not a normal approximation for cell counts in this context. Option D is incorrect; a high chi-square statistic indicates that the observed counts are *far* from the expected counts, suggesting a significant association. Option C is correct because the expected count for a cell is calculated as (row total * column total) / grand total, which directly uses the marginal proportions. For example, E_ij = (R_i * C_j) / N. This means E_ij = N * (R_i/N) * (C_j/N), where (R_i/N) and (C_j/N) are the marginal proportions for row i and column j, respectively.

A. 777

C. 999 ✓
D. 666
Correct Answer: (C) — 999
A two-way table is used to display the frequency distribution of two categorical variables. The number of rows corresponds to the categories of one variable, and the number of columns corresponds to the categories of the other variable. The total number of cells in the table is the product of the number of rows and the number of columns.

In this case, the number of rows is 3 (representing Dog, Cat, Fish) and the number of columns is 3 (representing Rock, Pop, Classical).

Total number of cells = Number of rows ×\times× Number of columns
Total number of cells = 3 ×\times× 3 = 9.

Each cell represents a unique combination of pet ownership and music genre, such as 'Dog' and 'Rock', 'Cat' and 'Pop', etc.
