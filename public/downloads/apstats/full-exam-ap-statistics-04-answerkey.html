- Factor 2: Watering frequency, with 444 different schedules.

If the experiment uses a completely randomized design where each subject receives a combination of one fertilizer formula and one watering schedule, how many distinct treatment groups are there?
A. 777
B. 646464
C. 121212 ✓
D. 242424
Correct Answer: (C) — 121212
In an experiment with multiple factors, a treatment is a specific combination of levels of each factor.

1. Identify the number of levels for each factor:
- Factor 1 (Fertilizer) has 333 levels.
- Factor 2 (Watering) has 444 levels.

2. Apply the multiplication counting principle to find the total number of distinct treatment combinations:
Number of treatments=3×4=12\text{Number of treatments} = 3 \times 4 = 12Number of treatments=3×4=12

- Choosing 777 is a common misconception resulting from adding the levels of the factors (3+43 + 43+4) rather than multiplying them.
- Choosing 646464 results from inappropriately raising one number of levels to the power of the other (434^343).
