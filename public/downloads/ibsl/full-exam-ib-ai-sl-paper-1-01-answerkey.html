The interquartile range (IQR) is defined as the difference between the third quartile (Q3) and the first quartile (Q1), i.e., IQR=Q3−Q1\text{IQR} = Q_3 - Q_1IQR=Q3​−Q1​. We are given that the IQR is 25.

The median of the dataset is given as 70. In a box plot, the median is represented by the line inside the box. Therefore, Q2=70Q_2 = 70Q2​=70.

The box plot also indicates that the third quartile (Q3) is 70. This is because the right edge of the box aligns with the median line, indicating Q3=70Q_3 = 70Q3​=70.

Now we can use the IQR formula to find Q1:
25=70−Q125 = 70 - Q_125=70−Q1​
Rearranging the equation to solve for Q1Q_1Q1​:
Q1=70−25Q_1 = 70 - 25Q1​=70−25
Q1=45Q_1 = 45Q1​=45

Therefore, the value of Q1 is 45. [M1] [A1]
