The quadratic function has roots at x=1x=1x=1 and x=5x=5x=5. Therefore, it can be written in the factored form f(x)=a(x−1)(x−5)f(x) = a(x-1)(x-5)f(x)=a(x−1)(x−5). We are given that the graph passes through the point (0,−10)(0, -10)(0,−10). Substituting these coordinates into the factored form:
−10=a(0−1)(0−5)-10 = a(0-1)(0-5)−10=a(0−1)(0−5)
−10=a(−1)(−5)-10 = a(-1)(-5)−10=a(−1)(−5)
−10=5a-10 = 5a−10=5a
Dividing both sides by 5, we get a=−2a = -2a=−2. [M1] [A1]
