(a) Write down the key mathematical relationship or initial equation required to model this problem. [2 marks]
(b) Hence, or otherwise, find the exact required analytical solution, showing all working clearly. [3 marks]
Official Examiner Mark Scheme [5 marks] — Final Answer: 151515
The histogram represents the frequency distribution of quiz scores. The total number of students is given as 50. The frequencies for the score intervals are as follows:
[50, 60): 8
[60, 70): 12
[70, 80): x
[80, 90): 7
[90, 100]: 8

The sum of the frequencies must equal the total number of students:
8 + 12 + x + 7 + 8 = 50

Combine the known frequencies:
35 + x = 50

Solve for x:
x = 50 - 35
x = 15

Therefore, the frequency for the score interval [70, 80) is 15. [M1] [A1]
