9 Geometry & Trigonometry Challenging [16 marks]
In the diagram, points A, B, and C form a triangle. Point D lies on AC such that BD is the angle bisector of angle ABC. Given that AB = 10 cm, AC = 15 cm, and BC = 12 cm, and that AD = 6 cm, find the length of DC.

(a) Write down the key mathematical relationship or initial equation required to model this problem. [6 marks]
(b) Hence, or otherwise, find the exact required analytical solution, showing all working clearly. [10 marks]
Official Examiner Mark Scheme [16 marks] — Final Answer: 7.2 cm
According to the Angle Bisector Theorem, an angle bisector of a triangle divides the opposite side into two segments that are proportional to the other two sides of the triangle. In triangle ABC, BD is the angle bisector of angle ABC. Therefore, we have the proportion:\nADDC=ABBC\frac{AD}{DC} = \frac{AB}{BC}DCAD​=BCAB​ \nWe are given that AB = 10 cm, AC = 15 cm, and BC = 12 cm. We are also given that AD = 6 cm. Let DC = x cm. \nSubstituting the given values into the proportion:\n6x=1012\frac{6}{x} = \frac{10}{12}x6​=1210​ \nTo solve for x, we can cross-multiply:\n6×12=10×x6 \times 12 = 10 \times x6×12=10×x \n72=10x72 = 10x72=10x \nx=7210x = \frac{72}{10}x=1072​ \nx=7.2x = 7.2x=7.2 \nThus, the length of DC is 7.2 cm.
