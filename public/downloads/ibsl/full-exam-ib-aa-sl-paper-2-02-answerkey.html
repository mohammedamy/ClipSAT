2 Statistics & Probability Foundational [5 marks]
The histogram below shows the number of hours 100 students spent studying for an exam. The number of students who studied for between 0 and 5 hours is represented by a bar with a height of 30. Determine the number of students who studied for between 5 and 10 hours.

(a) Write down the key mathematical relationship or initial equation required to model this problem. [2 marks]
(b) Hence, or otherwise, find the exact required analytical solution, showing all working clearly. [3 marks]
Official Examiner Mark Scheme [5 marks] — Final Answer: 202020
The histogram displays the distribution of study hours for 100 students. The total number of students is 100. The question states that the number of students who studied for between 0 and 5 hours is 30. The bar for the '0-5 hours' interval has a height of 30, representing 30 students. The bars for the '5-10 hours' and '10-15 hours' intervals have heights of 20 and 15 respectively, representing 20 and 15 students. Therefore, the number of students who studied for between 5 and 10 hours is 20. [M1] [A1]
