(b) Hence, or otherwise, find the exact required analytical solution, showing all working clearly. [3 marks]
Official Examiner Mark Scheme [5 marks] — Final Answer: 666
The yyy-intercept of a function is the value of yyy when x=0x=0x=0. For the function f(x)=−2x+6f(x) = -2x + 6f(x)=−2x+6, we substitute x=0x=0x=0 into the equation:

f(0)=−2(0)+6f(0) = -2(0) + 6f(0)=−2(0)+6
f(0)=0+6f(0) = 0 + 6f(0)=0+6
f(0)=6f(0) = 6f(0)=6

Therefore, the yyy-intercept is 6. [M1] [A1]
