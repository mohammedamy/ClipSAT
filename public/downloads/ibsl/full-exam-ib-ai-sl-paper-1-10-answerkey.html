The points where the graph intersects the x-axis are the roots of the quadratic equation f(x)=0f(x) = 0f(x)=0. We need to solve x2−4x+3=0x^2 - 4x + 3 = 0x2−4x+3=0. 7 AI SL — Specific Topics Standard [6 marks]
The graph of the quadratic function f(x)=ax2+bx+cf(x) = ax^2 + bx + cf(x)=ax2+bx+c is shown below. The vertex of the parabola is at (2, -4) and it passes through the point (4, 0). Determine the value of ccc.

(a) Write down the key mathematical relationship or initial equation required to model this problem. [2 marks]
(b) Hence, or otherwise, find the exact required analytical solution, showing all working clearly. [4 marks]
Official Examiner Mark Scheme [6 marks] — Final Answer: 000
The vertex form of a quadratic function is f(x)=a(x−h)2+kf(x) = a(x-h)^2 + kf(x)=a(x−h)2+k, where (h, k) is the vertex. Given the vertex is (2, -4), we have f(x)=a(x−2)2−4f(x) = a(x-2)^2 - 4f(x)=a(x−2)2−4. \\
Since the graph passes through the point (4, 0), we can substitute these values into the equation to find 'a': \\
0=a(4−2)2−40 = a(4-2)^2 - 40=a(4−2)2−4
0=a(2)2−40 = a(2)^2 - 40=a(2)2−4
0=4a−40 = 4a - 40=4a−4
4a=44a = 44a=4
a=1a = 1a=1
Therefore, the function is f(x)=(x−2)2−4f(x) = (x-2)^2 - 4f(x)=(x−2)2−4. \\
Expanding this equation to the form f(x)=ax2+bx+cf(x) = ax^2 + bx + cf(x)=ax2+bx+c: \\
f(x)=(x2−4x+4)−4f(x) = (x^2 - 4x + 4) - 4f(x)=(x2−4x+4)−4
f(x)=x2−4xf(x) = x^2 - 4xf(x)=x2−4x
Comparing this to f(x)=ax2+bx+cf(x) = ax^2 + bx + cf(x)=ax2+bx+c, we can see that a=1a=1a=1, b=−4b=-4b=−4, and c=0c=0c=0. \\
Thus, the value of ccc is 0. [M1] [A1]
