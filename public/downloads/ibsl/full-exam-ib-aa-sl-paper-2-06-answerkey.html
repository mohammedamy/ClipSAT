Thus, we need to solve f(x)=0f(x) = 0f(x)=0. \\ x2−4x+3=0x^2 - 4x + 3 = 0 \\x2−4x+3=0 Factoring the quadratic equation, we get (x−1)(x−3)=0(x-1)(x-3) = 0 \\(x−1)(x−3)=0 Therefore, the x-intercepts occur at x=1x=1x=1 and x=3x=3x=3.
