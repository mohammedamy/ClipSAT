4 Statistics & Probability Standard [6 marks]
The box plot below shows the distribution of scores for a class of 30 students on a recent test. The interquartile range (IQR) is 22 marks. The median score is 75 marks. The minimum score is 30 marks, and the maximum score is 98 marks.

Let the first quartile be denoted by Q1Q_1Q1​ and the third quartile be denoted by Q3Q_3Q3​.

If the upper whisker extends to 98 marks, and the third quartile Q3Q_3Q3​ is 86 marks, find the value of Q1Q_1Q1​.

(a) Write down the key mathematical relationship or initial equation required to model this problem. [2 marks]
(b) Hence, or otherwise, find the exact required analytical solution, showing all working clearly. [4 marks]
Official Examiner Mark Scheme [6 marks] — Final Answer: 646464
The interquartile range (IQR) is defined as the difference between the third quartile (Q3Q_3Q3​) and the first quartile (Q1Q_1Q1​), i.e., IQR=Q3−Q1\text{IQR} = Q_3 - Q_1IQR=Q3​−Q1​.
We are given that the IQR is 22 marks.
We are also given that the third quartile Q3=86Q_3 = 86Q3​=86 marks.
Substituting these values into the formula, we get:
22=86−Q122 = 86 - Q_122=86−Q1​
To find Q1Q_1Q1​, we rearrange the equation:
Q1=86−22Q_1 = 86 - 22Q1​=86−22
Q1=64Q_1 = 64Q1​=64
Therefore, the first quartile is 64 marks.

Common errors might include misinterpreting the median as Q1Q_1Q1​ or Q3Q_3Q3​, or incorrectly using the minimum or maximum values in the IQR calculation. [M1] [A1]
