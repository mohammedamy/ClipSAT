A. 150150150
B. 202020 ✓
C. 252525
D. 757575
Correct Answer: (B) — 202020
The Interquartile Range (IQR) is a measure of statistical dispersion, defined as the difference between the third quartile (Q3) and the first quartile (Q1).
IQR=Q3−Q1\text{IQR} = Q3 - Q1IQR=Q3−Q1
Substituting the given values:
IQR=85−65\text{IQR} = 85 - 65IQR=85−65
IQR=20\text{IQR} = 20IQR=20
Therefore, the interquartile range is 20.

50 Vectors & Matrices Challenging [1 mark]
In the figure below, triangle ABCABCABC is a right-angled triangle at BBB. Segment BDBDBD is the altitude to the hypotenuse ACACAC. If the length of AD=4AD = 4AD=4 cm and the length of DC=9DC = 9DC=9 cm, what is the length of the altitude BDBDBD, rounded to the nearest tenth?
A. 6.5 cm
B. 7.2 cm
C. 6.0 cm ✓
D. 3.7 cm
Correct Answer: (C) — 6.0 cm
According to the Geometric Mean Theorem for the altitude in a right-angled triangle, the square of the length of the altitude to the hypotenuse is equal to the product of the lengths of the two segments it divides the hypotenuse into. That is: BD2=AD×DCBD^2 = AD \times DCBD2=AD×DC.
Substituting the given values: BD2=4×9=36BD^2 = 4 \times 9 = 36BD2=4×9=36.
To find the length of BDBDBD, we take the square root of both sides: BD=36=6BD = \sqrt{36} = 6BD=36​=6 cm.
Rounding to the nearest tenth is not necessary as the result is an integer.
