P( > 10 | ) = 15 / 40.

We are looking for the conditional probability P(studies > 10 hours | Year 3).
Using the formula for conditional probability, we have: P(A|B) = P(A and B) / P(B).
In this case, A is the event "studies > 10 hours" and B is the event "Year 3".
From the table, the number of students who are in Year 3 (B) is 40.
The number of students who are in Year 3 AND study more than 10 hours (A and B) is 15.
Therefore, the conditional probability is the number of students in Year 3 who study more than 10 hours divided by the total number of students in Year 3.
P(studies > 10 hours | Year 3) = 15 / 40.
