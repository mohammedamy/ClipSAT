- Number of students who passed AC and QP: N(A∩B)=150N(A \cap B) = 150N(A∩B)=150

The question asks for the conditional probability that a student passed Quantum Physics given that they passed Advanced Calculus, which is denoted as P(B∣A)P(B|A)P(B∣A).

The formula for conditional probability is: P(B∣A)=P(A∩B)P(A)P(B|A) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(A)}P(B∣A)=P(A)P(A∩B)​

P(B∣A)=Number of students who passed both AC and QPNumber of students who passed ACP(B|A) = \frac{\text{Number of students who passed both AC and QP}}{\text{Number of students who passed AC}}P(B∣A)=Number of students who passed ACNumber of students who passed both AC and QP​

Using the values from the table:
P(B∣A)=150220P(B|A) = \frac{150}{220}P(B∣A)=220150​

frac150220=frac1522approx0.681818...\\frac{150}{220} = \\frac{15}{22} \\approx 0.681818...frac150220=frac1522approx0.681818...

Rounding to three decimal places, we get 0.682.

Therefore, the probability that a student also passed Quantum Physics, given that they passed Advanced Calculus, is approximately 0.682.
