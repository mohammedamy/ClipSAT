15 Problem-Solving and Data Analysis Standard [1 mark]
A data set of 20 distinct positive integers has a mean of 50 and a median of 45. If the largest number in the data set is increased by 100, which of the following statements must be true?
I. The mean increases.
II. The median increases.
III. The standard deviation increases.
A. I and III only
B. I, II, and III
C. I only
D. I and II only
Correct Answer: (A) — I and III only
Increasing the single largest value increases the total sum, so the mean increases (I is true). It does not affect the middle numbers, so the median does not change (II is false). It spreads the data further from the mean, increasing the standard deviation (III is true). Thus I and III only.
