15 Advanced Number Theory Foundational [1 mark]
What is the remainder when 41034^{103}4103 is divided by 111111?
A. 555
B. 999
C. 333
D. 444
Correct Answer: (B) — 999
We want to find the value of 4103(mod11)4^{103} \pmod{11}4103(mod11).

By Fermat's Little Theorem, if ppp is a prime number and gcd⁡(a,p)=1\gcd(a, p) = 1gcd(a,p)=1, then:
ap−1≡1(modp)a^{p-1} \equiv 1 \pmod pap−1≡1(modp)

Since 111111 is prime and gcd⁡(4,11)=1\gcd(4, 11) = 1gcd(4,11)=1, we have:
410≡1(mod11)4^{10} \equiv 1 \pmod{11}410≡1(mod11)

We express the exponent 103103103 in terms of multiples of 101010:
103=10×10+3103 = 10 \times 10 + 3103=10×10+3

Using exponent laws and modular arithmetic:
4103=(410)10×43≡(1)10×43≡43(mod11)4^{103} = (4^{10})^{10} \times 4^3 \equiv (1)^{10} \times 4^3 \equiv 4^3 \pmod{11}4103=(410)10×43≡(1)10×43≡43(mod11)

Now calculate 434^343:
43=644^3 = 6443=64
Dividing 646464 by 111111:
64=11×5+964 = 11 \times 5 + 964=11×5+9

Thus, the remainder is 999.

A common error is reducing the exponent modulo 111111 instead of modulo 101010 (or the multiplicative order 555), which incorrectly gives 103≡4(mod11)103 \equiv 4 \pmod{11}103≡4(mod11) and leads to 44≡34^4 \equiv 344≡3.
