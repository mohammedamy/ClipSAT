25 Vector Calculus Foundational [1 mark]
The figure shows a transformation grid. A point A is at (1,2)(1, 2)(1,2). After a transformation, the image of point A, denoted A', is at (−4,2)(-4, 2)(−4,2). Which of the following transformations maps A to A'?
A. A reflection across the y-axis
B. A rotation of 180 degrees about the origin
C. A dilation centered at the origin with a scale factor of 2
D. A translation by the vector ⟨−5,0⟩\langle -5, 0 \rangle⟨−5,0⟩
Correct Answer: (D) — A translation by the vector ⟨−5,0⟩\langle -5, 0 \rangle⟨−5,0⟩
Let the original point be A = (1, 2). The transformed point is A' = (-4, 2).

Option 1: Reflection across the y-axis maps (x, y) to (-x, y). So, (1, 2) would map to (-1, 2). This is not A'.

Option 2: A translation by the vector ⟨−5,0⟩\langle -5, 0 \rangle⟨−5,0⟩ means subtracting 5 from the x-coordinate and adding 0 to the y-coordinate. Applying this to A = (1, 2) gives (1 - 5, 2 + 0) = (-4, 2). This matches A'.

Option 3: A rotation of 180 degrees about the origin maps (x, y) to (-x, -y). So, (1, 2) would map to (-1, -2). This is not A'.

Option 4: A dilation centered at the origin with a scale factor of 2 maps (x, y) to (2x, 2y). So, (1, 2) would map to (2*1, 2*2) = (2, 4). This is not A'.

Therefore, the correct transformation is a translation by the vector ⟨−5,0⟩\langle -5, 0 \rangle⟨−5,0⟩.
