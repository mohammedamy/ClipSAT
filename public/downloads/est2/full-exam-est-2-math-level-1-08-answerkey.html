In the diagram below, triangle ABC is a right-angled triangle with the right angle at C. CD is the altitude to the hypotenuse AB. If AD = 4 units and DB = 9 units, find the length of the altitude CD.
A. 7.5 units
B. 13 units
C. 36 units ✓
D. 6 units
Correct Answer: (C) — 36 units
In a right-angled triangle, the altitude drawn to the hypotenuse creates three similar triangles: the original triangle and two smaller triangles. Specifically, triangle ADC is similar to triangle CDB. A property derived from this similarity is that the square of the length of the altitude to the hypotenuse is equal to the product of the lengths of the two segments it divides the hypotenuse into. This is known as the Geometric Mean Theorem. Therefore, CD2=AD×DBCD^2 = AD \times DBCD2=AD×DB. Given AD=4AD = 4AD=4 and DB=9DB = 9DB=9, we have CD2=4×9=36CD^2 = 4 \times 9 = 36CD2=4×9=36. Taking the square root of both sides, CD=36=6CD = \sqrt{36} = 6CD=36​=6 units.
