15 Trigonometry Foundational [1 mark]
In a right-angled triangle ABC, where angle B is 90 degrees, if AB = 3 and BC = 4, what is the length of the hypotenuse AC?
A. 777
B. 252525
C. 555
D. 121212
Correct Answer: (C) — 555
This question involves a right-angled triangle and requires the application of the Pythagorean theorem. The Pythagorean theorem states that in a right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse (the side opposite the right angle) is equal to the sum of the squares of the other two sides. In triangle ABC, AC is the hypotenuse. According to the theorem: AC2=AB2+BC2AC^2 = AB^2 + BC^2AC2=AB2+BC2 Substituting the given values: AC2=32+42AC^2 = 3^2 + 4^2AC2=32+42 AC2=9+16AC^2 = 9 + 16AC2=9+16 AC2=25AC^2 = 25AC2=25 To find the length of AC, we take the square root of both sides: AC=25AC = \sqrt{25}AC=25​ AC=5AC = 5AC=5 Therefore, the length of the hypotenuse AC is 5. Common errors might include simply adding the lengths of the two shorter sides (3 + 4 = 7) or calculating the square of the hypotenuse without taking the square root (25).
