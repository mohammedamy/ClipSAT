D. 555 cm

Let the height of the original cone be HHH and its radius be RRR. Let the height of the smaller cone be hhh and its radius be rrr. hH=rR\frac{h}{H} = \frac{r}{R} \\Hh​=Rr​

1218=r9\frac{12}{18} = \frac{r}{9} \\1812​=9r​

23=r9\frac{2}{3} = \frac{r}{9} \\32​=9r​
Multiply both sides by 9:

r=23×9r = \frac{2}{3} \times 9 \\r=32​×9
r=2×3r = 2 \times 3 \\r=2×3
r=6r = 6 \\r=6
Thus, the radius of the base of the smaller cone is 666 cm.
