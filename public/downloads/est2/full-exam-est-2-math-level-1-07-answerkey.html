The smaller cone is similar to the original cone, and its height is 121212 cm. If the radius of the base of the original cone is 999 cm, what is the radius of the base of the smaller cone?

C. 4.54.54.5 cm
D. 666 cm ✓

Let the height of the original cone be HHH and its radius be RRR. Let the height of the smaller cone be hhh and its radius be rrr. We are given that H=18H = 18H=18 cm, R=9R = 9R=9 cm, and h=12h = 12h=12 cm. Since the smaller cone is similar to the original cone, the ratio of their corresponding linear dimensions is constant. Therefore, the ratio of their heights is equal to the ratio of their radii:

hH=rR\frac{h}{H} = \frac{r}{R} \\Hh​=Rr​
Substituting the given values:

1218=r9\frac{12}{18} = \frac{r}{9} \\1812​=9r​

23=r9\frac{2}{3} = \frac{r}{9} \\32​=9r​
Multiply both sides by 9:

r=23×9r = \frac{2}{3} \times 9 \\r=32​×9
r=2×3r = 2 \times 3 \\r=2×3
r=6r = 6 \\r=6
Thus, the radius of the base of the smaller cone is 666 cm.
