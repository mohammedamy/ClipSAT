The diagram shows a right-angled triangle ABC, with the right angle at C. The altitude from C to the hypotenuse AB is CD. Given that AD=4AD = 4AD=4 and DB=9DB = 9DB=9, find the length of the altitude CD. [7]
Official Examiner Mark Scheme [7 marks] — Final Answer: 666
Mark Scheme (CAIE / Edexcel Grading Steps):
- Step 1 (Method & Formulation): Identify the required geometric/algebraic relationship from the diagram and substitute the given values. [M1]
- Step 2 (Derivation & Calculation): In a right-angled triangle, the altitude to the hypotenuse creates three similar triangles: △ABC∼△ACD∼△CBD\triangle ABC \sim \triangle ACD \sim \triangle CBD△ABC∼△ACD∼△CBD. Applying the geometric mean theorem for the altitude, we have CD2=AD×DBCD^2 = AD \times DBCD2=AD×DB. Given AD=4AD = 4AD=4 and DB=9DB = 9DB=9, we substitute these values into the formula: CD2=4×9=36CD^2 = 4 \times 9 = 36CD2=4×9=36. Taking the square root of both sides, CD=36=6CD = \sqrt{36} = 6CD=36​=6. Therefore, the length of the altitude CD is 6. [M1]
- Step 3 (Final Accuracy): Verified exact result: 6. [A1]
