Answer:
Official Examiner Mark Scheme [7 marks] — Final Answer: 58\frac{5}{8}85​
Mark Scheme (CAIE / Edexcel Grading Steps):
- Step 1 (Method & Formulation): Identify the required geometric/algebraic relationship from the diagram and substitute the given values. [M1]
- Step 2 (Derivation & Calculation): Let Y be the event that the yield is less than 50 kg, and B be the event that the fertilizer type is B. We are asked to find the conditional probability P(B|Y).

From the table, we have:
Number of plots with yield < 50 kg (event Y) = 80.
Number of plots with yield < 50 kg AND fertilizer type B (event B ∩\cap∩ Y) = 50.

The formula for conditional probability is P(B|Y) = P(B ∩\cap∩ Y) / P(Y).

Alternatively, using the counts directly:
P(B|Y) = (Number of plots with yield < 50 kg and fertilizer type B) / (Number of plots with yield < 50 kg)

P(B|Y) = 50 / 80

Simplifying the fraction:
P(B|Y) = 5/8.

Therefore, the probability that a randomly selected plot with a yield less than 50 kg received fertilizer type B is 5/8. [M1]
- Step 3 (Final Accuracy): Verified exact result: 5/8. [A1]
