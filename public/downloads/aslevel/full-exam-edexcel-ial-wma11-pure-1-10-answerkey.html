Official Examiner Mark Scheme [5 marks] — Final Answer: 6 cm
Mark Scheme (CAIE / Edexcel Grading Steps):
- Step 1 (Method & Formulation): Identify the required geometric/algebraic relationship from the diagram and substitute the given values. [M1]
- Step 2 (Derivation & Calculation): In a right-angled triangle, the altitude to the hypotenuse creates three similar triangles. Specifically, triangle ABD is similar to triangle CAD, and both are similar to the original triangle ABC. By the geometric mean theorem (or by using similarity ratios), the square of the altitude to the hypotenuse is equal to the product of the two segments it divides the hypotenuse into.
Therefore, AD2=BD×DCAD^2 = BD \times DCAD2=BD×DC.
Given BD=4BD = 4BD=4 cm and DC=9DC = 9DC=9 cm.
AD2=4×9AD^2 = 4 \times 9AD2=4×9
AD2=36AD^2 = 36AD2=36
AD=36AD = \sqrt{36}AD=36​
AD=6AD = 6AD=6 cm. [M1]
- Step 3 (Final Accuracy): Verified exact result: 6 cm. [A1]
