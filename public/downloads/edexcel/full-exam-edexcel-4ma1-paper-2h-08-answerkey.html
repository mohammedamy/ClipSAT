Official Examiner Mark Scheme [9 marks] — Final Answer: -22 and 8
Mark Scheme (CAIE / Edexcel Grading Steps):
- Step 1 (Method & Formulation): Identify the required geometric/algebraic relationship from the diagram and substitute the given values. [M1]
- Step 2 (Derivation & Calculation): Let the coordinate of point B be xxx. The distance between two points on a number line is the absolute difference of their coordinates. Therefore, the distance between A and B is ∣x−(−7)∣=∣x+7∣|x - (-7)| = |x + 7|∣x−(−7)∣=∣x+7∣. We are given that this distance is 151515 units. So, we have the equation ∣x+7∣=15|x + 7| = 15∣x+7∣=15. This gives us two possibilities: x+7=15x + 7 = 15x+7=15 or x+7=−15x + 7 = -15x+7=−15. Solving the first equation, x=15−7=8x = 15 - 7 = 8x=15−7=8. Solving the second equation, x=−15−7=−22x = -15 - 7 = -22x=−15−7=−22. Therefore, the possible coordinates of BBB are −22-22−22 and 888. [M1]
- Step 3 (Final Accuracy): Verified exact result: -22 and 8. [A1]
