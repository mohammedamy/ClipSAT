- Step 2 (Derivation & Calculation): Since AB is parallel to DE, we can identify two similar triangles: △ABC\triangle ABC△ABC and △DEC\triangle DEC△DEC. This is due to the property of alternate interior angles being equal when lines are parallel, and vertically opposite angles at C being equal.

Because the triangles are similar, the ratio of their corresponding sides is equal:
ABDE=ACCD=BCCE\frac{AB}{DE} = \frac{AC}{CD} = \frac{BC}{CE}DEAB​=CDAC​=CEBC​

We are given AC=6 cmAC = 6 \text{ cm}AC=6 cm, CD=9 cmCD = 9 \text{ cm}CD=9 cm, and DE=12 cmDE = 12 \text{ cm}DE=12 cm. We need to find AB.

AB12=69\frac{AB}{12} = \frac{6}{9}12AB​=96​

To solve for AB, multiply both sides by 12:
AB=69×12AB = \frac{6}{9} \times 12AB=96​×12
AB=23×12AB = \frac{2}{3} \times 12AB=32​×12
