Correct Answer: (A) — 10 units
This is a right-angled triangle, so we can use the Pythagorean theorem: a2+b2=c2a^2 + b^2 = c^2a2+b2=c2. Here, the sides are AB=8AB = 8AB=8 and BC=6BC = 6BC=6, and the hypotenuse is ACACAC.
So, AC2=AB2+BC2AC^2 = AB^2 + BC^2AC2=AB2+BC2.
AC2=82+62AC^2 = 8^2 + 6^2AC2=82+62
AC2=64+36AC^2 = 64 + 36AC2=64+36
AC2=100AC^2 = 100AC2=100
AC=100AC = \sqrt{100}AC=100​
AC=10AC = 10AC=10 units.

Common mistakes include adding the sides (8 + 6 = 14) or multiplying them (8 * 6 = 48) instead of applying the Pythagorean theorem.
