20 Polygons & Quadrilaterals Challenging [1 mark]
In isosceles trapezoid ABCDABCDABCD, base ABABAB is parallel to base CDCDCD with AB>CDAB > CDAB>CD. The midsegment MNMNMN connects the midpoint MMM of leg ADADAD to the midpoint NNN of leg BCBCBC. Diagonals ACACAC and BDBDBD intersect MNMNMN at points PPP and QQQ, respectively. If MN=16MN = 16MN=16, PQ=5PQ = 5PQ=5, and the leg length is AD=13AD = 13AD=13, what is the length of diagonal ACACAC?
A. 2612\sqrt{61}261​
B. 5175\sqrt{17}517​
C. 202020
D. 181818
Correct Answer: (C) — 202020
We solve the problem in three geometric steps using midsegment and isosceles trapezoid properties:

1. Determine the Base Lengths:
By the Trapezoid Midsegment Theorem, the midsegment MNMNMN has length:
MN=AB+CD2=16  ⟹  AB+CD=32MN = \frac{AB + CD}{2} = 16 \implies AB + CD = 32MN=2AB+CD​=16⟹AB+CD=32
In △ACD\triangle ACD△ACD, MPMPMP connects the midpoint of ADADAD parallel to base CDCDCD, so MP=12CDMP = \frac{1}{2}CDMP=21​CD. In △ABD\triangle ABD△ABD, MQMQMQ connects the midpoint of ADADAD parallel to base ABABAB, so MQ=12ABMQ = \frac{1}{2}ABMQ=21​AB. Therefore, the segment along the midsegment between the two diagonals is:
PQ=MQ−MP=AB−CD2=5  ⟹  AB−CD=10PQ = MQ - MP = \frac{AB - CD}{2} = 5 \implies AB - CD = 10PQ=MQ−MP=2AB−CD​=5⟹AB−CD=10
Solving the linear system:
AB=32+102=21,CD=32−102=11AB = \frac{32 + 10}{2} = 21, \quad CD = \frac{32 - 10}{2} = 11AB=232+10​=21,CD=232−10​=11

2. Determine the Height:
Dropping altitudes from DDD and CCC perpendicular to base ABABAB at D′D'D′ and C′C'C′, respectively, the projection of leg ADADAD onto ABABAB is:
AD′=AB−CD2=5AD' = \frac{AB - CD}{2} = 5AD′=2AB−CD​=5
Applying the Pythagorean theorem in right triangle △AD′D\triangle AD'D△AD′D:
(DD′)2=AD2−(AD′)2=132−52=169−25=144  ⟹  DD′=12(DD')^2 = AD^2 - (AD')^2 = 13^2 - 5^2 = 169 - 25 = 144 \implies DD' = 12(DD′)2=AD2−(AD′)2=132−52=169−25=144⟹DD′=12

3. Determine the Diagonal Length:
The remaining segment along the base from D′D'D′ to BBB has length:
D′B=AB−AD′=21−5=16D'B = AB - AD' = 21 - 5 = 16D′B=AB−AD′=21−5=16
Notice that for any isosceles trapezoid, D′BD'BD′B is equal to the midsegment length MNMNMN.
Applying the Pythagorean theorem in right triangle △BD′D\triangle BD'D△BD′D:
BD=(DD′)2+(D′B)2=122+162=144+256=400=20BD = \sqrt{(DD')^2 + (D'B)^2} = \sqrt{12^2 + 16^2} = \sqrt{144 + 256} = \sqrt{400} = 20BD=(DD′)2+(D′B)2​=122+162​=144+256​=400​=20
Since the diagonals of an isosceles trapezoid are congruent, AC=BD=20AC = BD = 20AC=BD=20.
