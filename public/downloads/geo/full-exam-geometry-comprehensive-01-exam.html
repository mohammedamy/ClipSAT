Point MMM is the midpoint of segment AB‾\overline{AB}AB. If AM=3x+1AM = 3x + 1AM=3x+1 and MB=5x−9MB = 5x - 9MB=5x−9, what is the length of segment AB‾\overline{AB}AB?

B. 323232
C. 262626
D. 555
Correct Answer: (B) — 323232
1. Apply the definition of a midpoint:
Since MMM is the midpoint of segment AB‾\overline{AB}AB, the two sub-segments have equal lengths:
AM=MBAM = MBAM=MB
Substitute the given algebraic expressions:
3x+1=5x−93x + 1 = 5x - 93x+1=5x−9

2. Solve for xxx:
Subtract 3x3x3x from both sides:
1=2x−91 = 2x - 91=2x−9
Add 999 to both sides:
