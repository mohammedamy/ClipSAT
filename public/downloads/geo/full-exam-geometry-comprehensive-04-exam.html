15 Lines & Angles Standard [1 mark]
Two parallel lines, ppp and qqq, are intersected by a transversal line ttt. A pair of consecutive interior angles have measures (4x+9)∘(4x + 9)^\circ(4x+9)∘ and (2x+33)∘(2x + 33)^\circ(2x+33)∘. What is the value of xxx?
A. 373737
B. 121212
C. 232323
D. 888
Correct Answer: (C) — 232323
By the Consecutive Interior Angles Theorem, when two parallel lines are cut by a transversal, consecutive interior angles are supplementary (their measures sum to 180∘180^\circ180∘).

Set up the equation:
(4x+9)+(2x+33)=180(4x + 9) + (2x + 33) = 180(4x+9)+(2x+33)=180

Combine like terms:
6x+42=1806x + 42 = 1806x+42=180

Subtract 424242 from both sides:
6x=1386x = 1386x=138

Divide by 666:
x=23x = 23x=23

Evaluating the angle measures at x=23x = 23x=23 gives 4(23)+9=101∘4(23) + 9 = 101^\circ4(23)+9=101∘ and 2(23)+33=79∘2(23) + 33 = 79^\circ2(23)+33=79∘, which are supplementary (101∘+79∘=180∘101^\circ + 79^\circ = 180^\circ101∘+79∘=180∘).

Common Misconceptions:
- Choice A (888): Mistakenly assumes consecutive interior angles are complementary, solving 6x+42=906x + 42 = 906x+42=90.
- Choice C (121212): Confuses consecutive interior angles with alternate interior angles and sets them equal, solving 4x+9=2x+334x + 9 = 2x + 334x+9=2x+33.
- Choice D (373737): Makes an algebraic sign error by adding 424242 to 180180180 instead of subtracting, solving 6x=2226x = 2226x=222.
