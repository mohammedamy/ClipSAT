19 Statistics & Probability [1 mark]
A box contains 10 cards, each labeled with a distinct integer from 1 through 10. If 3 cards are drawn at random from the box without replacement, what is the probability that the sum of the numbers on the 3 drawn cards is divisible by 3?
A. 310\dfrac{3}{10}103​
B. 13\dfrac{1}{3}31​
C. 720\dfrac{7}{20}207​ ✓
D. 14\dfrac{1}{4}41​
Correct Answer: (C) — 720\dfrac{7}{20}207​
To find the probability that the sum of the 3 chosen integers is divisible by 3, we synthesize combinatorial counting with modular arithmetic.

1. Total Number of Outcomes:
The total number of ways to choose 3 cards from 10 without replacement is given by the combination formula:
(103)=10×9×83×2×1=120\binom{10}{3} = \frac{10 \times 9 \times 8}{3 \times 2 \times 1} = 120(310​)=3×2×110×9×8​=120

2. Partition by Remainder modulo 3:
Group the integers 1 through 10 by their remainder when divided by 3:
- Remainder 0: {3,6,9}\{3, 6, 9\}{3,6,9} (3 cards)
- Remainder 1: {1,4,7,10}\{1, 4, 7, 10\}{1,4,7,10} (4 cards)
- Remainder 2: {2,5,8}\{2, 5, 8\}{2,5,8} (3 cards)

3. Favorable Combinations:
The sum of three integers is divisible by 3 if and only if the sum of their remainders modulo 3 is a multiple of 3. There are only two ways this can occur:
- Subcase A: All 3 cards have the same remainder modulo 3:
- Three cards with remainder 0: (33)=1\binom{3}{3} = 1(33​)=1
- Three cards with remainder 1: (43)=4\binom{4}{3} = 4(34​)=4
- Three cards with remainder 2: (33)=1\binom{3}{3} = 1(33​)=1
Total for this subcase: 1+4+1=61 + 4 + 1 = 61+4+1=6 outcomes.

- Subcase B: Exactly one card from each remainder group (remainders 0, 1, and 2):
- (31)×(41)×(31)=3×4×3=36\binom{3}{1} \times \binom{4}{1} \times \binom{3}{1} = 3 \times 4 \times 3 = 36(13​)×(14​)×(13​)=3×4×3=36 outcomes.

4. Total Favorable Outcomes and Probability:
Adding all favorable outcomes yields:
6+36=426 + 36 = 426+36=42
Therefore, the probability is:
P=42120=720P = \frac{42}{120} = \frac{7}{20}P=12042​=207​

Common Misconceptions:
- Guessing 13\frac{1}{3}31​: It is tempting to assume that sums are equally distributed among the three possible remainders modulo 3. However, because the remainder groups do not have equal cardinality (remainder 1 has 4 members while the others have 3) and sampling is without replacement, the distribution is not uniform.
- Obtaining 310\frac{3}{10}103​: This occurs if one considers only Subcase B (one of each remainder, 36/12036/12036/120) and overlooks the combinations where all three cards share the same remainder.
