To find the equivalent expression, expand (2x−3)(4x+1)(2x - 3)(4x + 1)(2x−3)(4x+1) by applying the distributive property (FOIL method):

1. Multiply the First terms: (2x)(4x)=8x2(2x)(4x) = 8x^2(2x)(4x)=8x2
2. Multiply the Outer terms: (2x)(1)=2x(2x)(1) = 2x(2x)(1)=2x
3. Multiply the Inner terms: (−3)(4x)=−12x(-3)(4x) = -12x(−3)(4x)=−12x
4. Multiply the Last terms: (−3)(1)=−3(-3)(1) = -3(−3)(1)=−3

Now, sum these products and combine like terms:
(2x−3)(4x+1)=8x2+2x−12x−3=8x2−10x−3(2x - 3)(4x + 1) = 8x^2 + 2x - 12x - 3 = 8x^2 - 10x - 3(2x−3)(4x+1)=8x2+2x−12x−3=8x2−10x−3

Common Errors:
- 8x2−38x^2 - 38x2−3 incorrectly omits the inner and outer cross-terms.
- 8x2−14x−38x^2 - 14x - 38x2−14x−3 occurs when combining the middle terms as −12x−2x-12x - 2x−12x−2x instead of −12x+2x-12x + 2x−12x+2x.

B. −4-4−4
C. 444
D. 222
Correct Answer: (A) — −2-2−2
To solve for xxx and yyy from a single equation with real variables, we can complete the square for both xxx and yyy:

1. Group the xxx-terms and the yyy-terms:
(2x2−4x)+(y2+6y)+11=0(2x^2 - 4x) + (y^2 + 6y) + 11 = 0(2x2−4x)+(y2+6y)+11=0

2. Factor out the leading coefficient from the xxx-terms:
