Official Examiner Mark Scheme [5 marks] — Final Answer: 76 units s−176\text{ units s}^{-1}76 units s−1
To find the rate of change of yyy with respect to time ttt, we apply the chain rule for connected rates of change:
dydt=dydx×dxdt\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t} = \frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} \times \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}dtdy​=dxdy​×dtdx​
First, differentiate yyy with respect to xxx:
dydx=ddx(2x3−5x)=6x2−5\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}(2x^3 - 5x) = 6x^2 - 5dxdy​=dxd​(2x3−5x)=6x2−5
Substitute x=2x = 2x=2 into the derivative:
dydx∣x=2=6(2)2−5=24−5=19\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}\Big|_{x=2} = 6(2)^2 - 5 = 24 - 5 = 19dxdy​​x=2​=6(2)2−5=24−5=19
Given that dxdt=4 units s−1\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} = 4\text{ units s}^{-1}dtdx​=4 units s−1, we calculate:
dydt=19×4=76 units s−1\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t} = 19 \times 4 = 76\text{ units s}^{-1}dtdy​=19×4=76 units s−1
Common errors:

- 28 units s−128\text{ units s}^{-1}28 units s−1 results from incorrectly differentiating 2x32x^32x3 as 6x6x6x, giving (6(2)−5)×4=28(6(2) - 5) \times 4 = 28(6(2)−5)×4=28.
