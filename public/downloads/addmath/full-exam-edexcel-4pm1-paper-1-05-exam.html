Official Examiner Mark Scheme [5 marks] — Final Answer: −3-3−3
Method & Working: According to the Remainder Theorem, when a polynomial P(x)\mathrm{P}(x)P(x) is divided by x−cx - cx−c, the remainder is equal to P(c)\mathrm{P}(c)P(c).

Here, the divisor is x−3x - 3x−3, so c=3c = 3c=3. We evaluate P(3)\mathrm{P}(3)P(3):
P(3)=2(3)3−3(3)2+a(3)−8\mathrm{P}(3) = 2(3)^3 - 3(3)^2 + a(3) - 8P(3)=2(3)3−3(3)2+a(3)−8
P(3)=2(27)−3(9)+3a−8=54−27+3a−8=19+3a\mathrm{P}(3) = 2(27) - 3(9) + 3a - 8 = 54 - 27 + 3a - 8 = 19 + 3aP(3)=2(27)−3(9)+3a−8=54−27+3a−8=19+3a

Given that the remainder is 101010:
19+3a=1019 + 3a = 1019+3a=10
3a=10−19=−93a = 10 - 19 = -93a=10−19=−9
a=−3a = -3a=−3

Common misconceptions:
- Choosing 333 results from a sign error when rearranging 19+3a=1019 + 3a = 1019+3a=10 to 3a=19−10=93a = 19 - 10 = 93a=19−10=9.
- Choosing −9-9−9 results from stopping at 3a=−93a = -93a=−9 without dividing by the coefficient of aaa.
- Choosing −33-33−33 results from evaluating P(−3)\mathrm{P}(-3)P(−3) instead of P(3)\mathrm{P}(3)P(3). [M1][A1]
Final Verified Answer: −3-3−3 [A1]
