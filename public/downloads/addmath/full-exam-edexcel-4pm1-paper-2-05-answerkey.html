6 Permutations & Combinations Standard [7 marks]
A committee of 5 members is to be selected from a group consisting of nnn men and 6 women, where n≥4n \ge 4n≥4.

Given that the number of possible committees containing exactly 3 men is equal to the number of possible committees containing exactly 4 men, find the value of nnn. [7]
Official Examiner Mark Scheme [7 marks] — Final Answer: 131313
To form a committee of 5 members:

1. Committees with exactly 3 men:
Since there are 5 members in total, the committee must contain 3 men and 5−3=25 - 3 = 25−3=2 women.
Number of ways=(n3)×(62)=(n3)×6×52=15(n3)\text{Number of ways} = \binom{n}{3} \times \binom{6}{2} = \binom{n}{3} \times \frac{6 \times 5}{2} = 15\binom{n}{3}Number of ways=(3n​)×(26​)=(3n​)×26×5​=15(3n​)

2. Committees with exactly 4 men:
The committee must contain 4 men and 5−4=15 - 4 = 15−4=1 woman.
Number of ways=(n4)×(61)=6(n4)\text{Number of ways} = \binom{n}{4} \times \binom{6}{1} = 6\binom{n}{4}Number of ways=(4n​)×(16​)=6(4n​)

3. Equating the two expressions:
15(n3)=6(n4)15\binom{n}{3} = 6\binom{n}{4}15(3n​)=6(4n​)
Divide both sides by 3:
5(n3)=2(n4)5\binom{n}{3} = 2\binom{n}{4}5(3n​)=2(4n​)
Using the relation (n4)=n−34(n3)\binom{n}{4} = \frac{n - 3}{4}\binom{n}{3}(4n​)=4n−3​(3n​):
5(n3)=2×n−34(n3)=n−32(n3)5\binom{n}{3} = 2 \times \frac{n - 3}{4}\binom{n}{3} = \frac{n - 3}{2}\binom{n}{3}5(3n​)=2×4n−3​(3n​)=2n−3​(3n​)
Since n≥4n \ge 4n≥4, (n3)≠0\binom{n}{3} \neq 0(3n​)=0, so we can divide both sides by (n3)\binom{n}{3}(3n​):
5=n−32  ⟹  10=n−3  ⟹  n=135 = \frac{n - 3}{2} \implies 10 = n - 3 \implies n = 135=2n−3​⟹10=n−3⟹n=13

Common Traps:
- Setting (n3)=(n4)\binom{n}{3} = \binom{n}{4}(3n​)=(4n​) ignores the selection of the women needed to complete the 5-member committee, giving n−34=1  ⟹  n=7\frac{n-3}{4} = 1 \implies n = 74n−3​=1⟹n=7.
