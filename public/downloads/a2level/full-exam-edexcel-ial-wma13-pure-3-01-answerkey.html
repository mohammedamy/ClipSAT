Official Examiner Mark Scheme [5 marks] — Final Answer: 222
To find the gradient of the tangent to the curve y=ln⁡(2x3+1)y = \ln(2x^3 + 1)y=ln(2x3+1) at x=1x = 1x=1, we differentiate yyy with respect to xxx using the chain rule.

Let u=2x3+1u = 2x^3 + 1u=2x3+1, so y=ln⁡(u)y = \ln(u)y=ln(u).
Then:
dudx=6x2anddydu=1u\dfrac{\mathrm{d}u}{\mathrm{d}x} = 6x^2 \quad \text{and} \quad \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}u} = \dfrac{1}{u}dxdu​=6x2anddudy​=u1​
Applying the chain rule:
dydx=dydu⋅dudx=6x22x3+1\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}u} \cdot \dfrac{\mathrm{d}u}{\mathrm{d}x} = \dfrac{6x^2}{2x^3 + 1}dxdy​=dudy​⋅dxdu​=2x3+16x2​
Now evaluate the derivative at x=1x = 1x=1:
dydx∣x=1=6(1)22(1)3+1=63=2\left.\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}\right|_{x=1} = \dfrac{6(1)^2}{2(1)^3 + 1} = \dfrac{6}{3} = 2dxdy​​x=1​=2(1)3+16(1)2​=36​=2

- Choice C (23\frac{2}{3}32​) arises from an incorrect derivative of the inner polynomial, such as differentiating 2x32x^32x3 incorrectly as 2x2x2x.
