2 Hypothesis Testing Foundational [5 marks]
In a hypothesis test at a fixed significance level, the probability of committing a Type II error for a particular alternative value of the parameter is β=0.18\beta = 0.18β=0.18. What is the power of the test against this alternative? [5]
Official Examiner Mark Scheme [5 marks] — Final Answer: 0.820.820.82
Method & Working: The power of a hypothesis test is the probability of correctly rejecting the null hypothesis H0\mathrm{H}_0H0​ when a given alternative parameter value is true. Since a Type II error is the failure to reject H0\mathrm{H}_0H0​ when that alternative is true (with probability β\betaβ), the power of the test is the complementary probability 1−β=1−0.18=0.821 - \beta = 1 - 0.18 = 0.821−β=1−0.18=0.82. [M1][A1]
Final Verified Answer: 0.820.820.82 [A1]
