1 Sampling & Estimation Foundational [5 marks]

Official Examiner Mark Scheme [5 marks] — Final Answer: 484848
Method & Working: Since P(T∩U)=0.6×0.8=0.48P(T \cap U) = 0.6 \times 0.8 = 0.48P(T∩U)=0.6×0.8=0.48, the expected count in 100 trials is 484848. [M1][A1]
