8 Statistics & Probability (HL) Foundational [14 marks]
The table below shows the results of a survey on preferred music genres among a group of students. What is the relative frequency of students who prefer rock music?

(a) Write down the key mathematical relationship or initial equation required to model this problem. [5 marks]
(b) Hence, or otherwise, find the exact required analytical solution, showing all working clearly. [9 marks]
Official Examiner Mark Scheme [14 marks] — Final Answer: 0.250.250.25
To find the relative frequency of students who prefer rock music, we first need to find the total number of students surveyed. The total number of students is the sum of students who prefer each genre: Pop (10) + Rock (15) + Hip-Hop (20) + Electronic (15) = 60 students.

The number of students who prefer rock music is 15.

The relative frequency is calculated as: (Number of students who prefer rock music) / (Total number of students).
Relative Frequency (Rock) = 15 / 60 = 1/4 = 0.25. [M1] [A1]

(a) Write down the key mathematical relationship or initial equation required to model this problem. [6 marks]
(b) Hence, or otherwise, find the exact required analytical solution, showing all working clearly. [10 marks]
Official Examiner Mark Scheme [16 marks] — Final Answer: 303030
The histogram shows the frequency distribution of study hours. The total number of students surveyed is 120.

The number of students in each interval is represented by the height of the bars multiplied by the width of the interval.

Interval (0, 1]: Frequency = 15
Interval (1, 2]: Frequency = 25
Interval (2, 3]: Frequency = 30
Interval (3, 4]: Frequency = x
Interval (4, 5]: Frequency = 20

The sum of the frequencies must equal the total number of students:
15+25+30+x+20=12015 + 25 + 30 + x + 20 = 12015+25+30+x+20=120
90+x=12090 + x = 12090+x=120
x=120−90x = 120 - 90x=120−90
x=30x = 30x=30

Therefore, 30 students studied for more than 3 hours but at most 4 hours. [M1] [A1]
