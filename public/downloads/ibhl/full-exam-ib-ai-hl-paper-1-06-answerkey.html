10 Vectors & 3D Geometry Challenging [9 marks]
Consider the tetrahedron OABC with vertices O(0, 0, 0), A(2, 1, 3), B(1, 2, -1), and C(k, k+1, k-1). Given that the volume of the tetrahedron is 7 cubic units, find the possible values of k.

(a) Write down the key mathematical relationship or initial equation required to model this problem. [3 marks]
(b) Hence, or otherwise, find the exact required analytical solution, showing all working clearly. [6 marks]
Official Examiner Mark Scheme [9 marks] — Final Answer: k = 2 or k = 3
The volume of a tetrahedron with vertices O(0,0,0), A, B, C is given by the formula V=16∣det⁡(OA⃗,OB⃗,OC⃗)∣V = \frac{1}{6} | \det(\vec{OA}, \vec{OB}, \vec{OC}) |V=61​∣det(OA,OB,OC)∣.
