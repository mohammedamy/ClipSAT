The diagram shows the graph of a quadratic function y=f(x)=x2−6x+ky = f(x) = x^2 - 6x + ky=f(x)=x2−6x+k. The graph intersects the x-axis at point A, and has a minimum point at B. The coordinates of point A are (3,0)(3, 0)(3,0). Find the value of kkk.

(a) Write down the key mathematical relationship or initial equation required to model this problem. [10 marks]
(b) Hence, or otherwise, find the exact required analytical solution, showing all working clearly. [17 marks]
Official Examiner Mark Scheme [27 marks] — Final Answer: 999
The function is given by f(x)=x2−6x+kf(x) = x^2 - 6x + kf(x)=x2−6x+k. We are given that the graph intersects the x-axis at point A (3,0)(3, 0)(3,0). This means that when x=3x=3x=3, f(x)=0f(x)=0f(x)=0. Substituting these values into the function's equation:
0=(3)2−6(3)+k0 = (3)^2 - 6(3) + k0=(3)2−6(3)+k
0=9−18+k0 = 9 - 18 + k0=9−18+k
0=−9+k0 = -9 + k0=−9+k
k=9k = 9k=9

Therefore, the value of kkk is 9.
