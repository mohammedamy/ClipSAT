To find the total number of students surveyed, we need to sum the frequencies for each category of hours studied.
Total students = Frequency(0 ≤ h < 5) + Frequency(5 ≤ h < 10) + Frequency(10 ≤ h < 15) + Frequency(15 ≤ h < 20)
