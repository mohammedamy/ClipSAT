(a) Write down the key mathematical relationship or initial equation required to model this problem. [2 marks]
(b) Hence, or otherwise, find the exact required analytical solution, showing all working clearly. [5 marks]
Official Examiner Mark Scheme [7 marks] — Final Answer: ±1\pm 1±1
The modulus of a complex number z=x+yiz = x + yiz=x+yi is given by ∣z∣=x2+y2|z| = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}∣z∣=x2+y2​.
We are given z=a+5iz = a + 5iz=a+5i and ∣z∣=26|z| = \sqrt{26}∣z∣=26​.
Therefore, ∣z∣=a2+52=a2+25|z| = \sqrt{a^2 + 5^2} = \sqrt{a^2 + 25}∣z∣=a2+52​=a2+25​.
We are given that ∣z∣=26|z| = \sqrt{26}∣z∣=26​.
So, we have the equation: a2+25=26\sqrt{a^2 + 25} = \sqrt{26}a2+25​=26​.
Squaring both sides gives: a2+25=26a^2 + 25 = 26a2+25=26.
Subtracting 25 from both sides, we get: a2=1a^2 = 1a2=1.
Taking the square root of both sides, we find: a=±1a = \pm 1a=±1.

Common mistakes include: ignoring the fact that aaa can be negative, or incorrectly calculating the modulus. [M1] [A1]
