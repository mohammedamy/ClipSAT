8 Statistics & Probability (HL) [5 marks]
Art
Not Art
A school offers two optional subjects: Art and Music. The number of students enrolled in each subject and the number of students enrolled in both is shown in the Venn diagram below. The total number of students in the school is 120. Let A be the set of students enrolled in Art and M be the set of students enrolled in Music.

Given that n(A∪M)=90n(A \cup M) = 90n(A∪M)=90, find the number of students enrolled in neither Art nor Music.

(a) Write down the key mathematical relationship or initial equation required to model this problem. [2 marks]
(b) Hence, or otherwise, find the exact required analytical solution, showing all working clearly. [3 marks]
Official Examiner Mark Scheme [5 marks] — Final Answer: 303030
The Venn diagram shows that n(A∩M)=20n(A \cap M) = 20n(A∩M)=20, n(A∖M)=3xn(A \setminus M) = 3xn(A∖M)=3x, and n(M∖A)=4xn(M \setminus A) = 4xn(M∖A)=4x. The total number of students in the school is 120. We are given that n(A∪M)=90n(A \cup M) = 90n(A∪M)=90. The formula for the union of two sets is n(A∪M)=n(A∖M)+n(M∖A)+n(A∩M)n(A \cup M) = n(A \setminus M) + n(M \setminus A) + n(A \cap M)n(A∪M)=n(A∖M)+n(M∖A)+n(A∩M).
Substituting the given values: 90=3x+4x+2090 = 3x + 4x + 2090=3x+4x+20.
Subtracting 20 from both sides: 70=7x70 = 7x70=7x.
Dividing by 7: x=10x = 10x=10.

Now we can find the number of students in Art only and Music only:
n(A∖M)=3x=3(10)=30n(A \setminus M) = 3x = 3(10) = 30n(A∖M)=3x=3(10)=30.
n(M∖A)=4x=4(10)=40n(M \setminus A) = 4x = 4(10) = 40n(M∖A)=4x=4(10)=40.

The number of students enrolled in Art or Music or both is n(A∪M)=30+40+20=90n(A \cup M) = 30 + 40 + 20 = 90n(A∪M)=30+40+20=90, which matches the given information.

The number of students enrolled in neither Art nor Music is the total number of students minus the number of students enrolled in at least one of the subjects:
Number of students in neither = Total students - n(A∪M)n(A \cup M)n(A∪M)
Number of students in neither = 120−90=30120 - 90 = 30120−90=30.

Therefore, 30 students are enrolled in neither Art nor Music. [M1] [A1]
