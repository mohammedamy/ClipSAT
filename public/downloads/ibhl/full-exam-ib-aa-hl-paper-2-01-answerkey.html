The parabola passes through the point (−1,0)(-1, 0)(−1,0). Substituting these coordinates into the equation:
0=a(−1−1)2+40 = a(-1-1)^2 + 40=a(−1−1)2+4
0=a(−2)2+40 = a(-2)^2 + 40=a(−2)2+4
0=4a+40 = 4a + 40=4a+4
4a=−44a = -44a=−4
a=−1a = -1a=−1.

So, the function is f(x)=−(x−1)2+4f(x) = -(x-1)^2 + 4f(x)=−(x−1)2+4. Expanding this:
f(x)=−(x2−2x+1)+4f(x) = -(x^2 - 2x + 1) + 4f(x)=−(x2−2x+1)+4
