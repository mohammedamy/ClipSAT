1 Calculus (HL) [4 marks]
The linear equation 17(v−13)+7(v−13)=24017(v - 13) + 7(v - 13) = 24017(v−13)+7(v−13)=240 holds for real vvv. Find the solution vvv of U(v)=240U(v) = 240U(v)=240.

(a) Write down the key mathematical relationship or initial equation required to model this problem. [2 marks]
(b) Hence, or otherwise, find the exact required analytical solution, showing all working clearly. [2 marks]
Official Examiner Mark Scheme [4 marks] — Final Answer: 232323
Factoring gives (17+7)(v−13)=240  ⟹  v=23(17 + 7)(v - 13) = 240 \implies v = 23(17+7)(v−13)=240⟹v=23. [M1] [A1]
