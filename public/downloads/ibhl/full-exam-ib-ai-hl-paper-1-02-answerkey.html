1 Statistics & Probability (HL) Foundational [6 marks]
0 \le h < 5
5 \le h < 10
10 \le h < 15
15 \le h < 20
A small study was conducted on the number of hours students spent studying per week. The results are summarized in the frequency table below:

What is the total number of students surveyed?

(a) Write down the key mathematical relationship or initial equation required to model this problem. [2 marks]
(b) Hence, or otherwise, find the exact required analytical solution, showing all working clearly. [4 marks]
Official Examiner Mark Scheme [6 marks] — Final Answer: 707070
To find the total number of students surveyed, we need to sum the frequencies for each category of hours studied.
Total students = Frequency(0 ≤ h < 5) + Frequency(5 ≤ h < 10) + Frequency(10 ≤ h < 15) + Frequency(15 ≤ h < 20)
Total students = 12 + 25 + 18 + 15
Total students = 70
Therefore, the total number of students surveyed is 70. [M1] [A1]
