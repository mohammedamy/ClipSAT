Triangle ABCABCABC is mapped to triangle A′B′C′A'B'C'A′B′C′ on the coordinate plane as shown below.

Which coordinate rule represents this transformation?
A. (x,y)→(x−5,y)(x, y) \to (x - 5, y)(x,y)→(x−5,y)
B. (x,y)→(−x,y)(x, y) \to (-x, y)(x,y)→(−x,y)

Correct Answer: (A) — (x,y)→(x−5,y)(x, y) \to (x - 5, y)(x,y)→(x−5,y)
To determine the transformation rule, compare the coordinates of corresponding vertices of triangle ABCABCABC and triangle A′B′C′A'B'C'A′B′C′:
- Vertex A(1,2)A(1, 2)A(1,2) maps to A′(−4,2)A'(-4, 2)A′(−4,2).
- Vertex B(4,2)B(4, 2)B(4,2) maps to B′(−1,2)B'(-1, 2)B′(−1,2).
- Vertex C(2,5)C(2, 5)C(2,5) maps to C′(−3,5)C'(-3, 5)C′(−3,5).

For each vertex, the change in the xxx-coordinate is:
Δx=−4−1=−5\Delta x = -4 - 1 = -5Δx=−4−1=−5
The yyy-coordinate remains unchanged:
Δy=2−2=0\Delta y = 2 - 2 = 0Δy=2−2=0

This is a horizontal translation of 5 units to the left, which is represented by the transformation rule (x,y)→(x−5,y)(x, y) \to (x - 5, y)(x,y)→(x−5,y).

- (x,y)→(x+5,y)(x, y) \to (x + 5, y)(x,y)→(x+5,y) represents a translation of 5 units to the right.
- (x,y)→(x,y−5)(x, y) \to (x, y - 5)(x,y)→(x,y−5) represents a vertical translation of 5 units down.
- (x,y)→(−x,y)(x, y) \to (-x, y)(x,y)→(−x,y) represents a reflection across the yyy-axis, which would send A(1,2)A(1, 2)A(1,2) to (−1,2)(-1, 2)(−1,2), not (−4,2)(-4, 2)(−4,2).
