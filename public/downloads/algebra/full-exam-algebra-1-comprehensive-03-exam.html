B. 171717
C. 999
D. 444
Correct Answer: (A) — 131313
By the Geometric Mean Theorem (Altitude Rule) for right triangles, the altitude to the hypotenuse is the geometric mean of the two segments of the hypotenuse:

CD2=AD⋅DBCD^2 = AD \cdot DBCD2=AD⋅DB

Substitute the given values into the equation:
62=x(x+5)6^2 = x(x + 5)62=x(x+5)
36=x2+5x36 = x^2 + 5x36=x2+5x

Rearrange into standard quadratic form:
x2+5x−36=0x^2 + 5x - 36 = 0x2+5x−36=0

Factor the quadratic equation:
(x+9)(x−4)=0(x + 9)(x - 4) = 0(x+9)(x−4)=0

This gives two solutions: x=−9x = -9x=−9 and x=4x = 4x=4. Because AD=xAD = xAD=x represents a geometric length, it must be positive. Thus, we reject the extraneous negative root x=−9x = -9x=−9, which leaves x=4x = 4x=4.

15 Factoring Polynomials Standard [1 mark]
The polynomial P(x)=2x4−7x3+ax2+bx−12P(x) = 2x^4 - 7x^3 + ax^2 + bx - 12P(x)=2x4−7x3+ax2+bx−12 has x2−4x^2 - 4x2−4 as a factor, where aaa and bbb are real constants. Which of the following is also a factor of P(x)P(x)P(x)?
A. 2x−12x - 12x−1
B. x+3x + 3x+3
C. 2x−32x - 32x−3
D. 2x+12x + 12x+1
Correct Answer: (A) — 2x−12x - 12x−1
We can solve this problem using either polynomial structure inspection or the Factor Theorem.

Method 1: Polynomial Structure & Division
Since x2−4x^2 - 4x2−4 is a factor of P(x)P(x)P(x), the quotient Q(x)Q(x)Q(x) must be a quadratic polynomial such that:
P(x)=(x2−4)Q(x)P(x) = (x^2 - 4) Q(x)P(x)=(x2−4)Q(x)
To match the leading term 2x42x^42x4 and the constant term −12-12−12, Q(x)Q(x)Q(x) must be of the form 2x2+kx+32x^2 + kx + 32x2+kx+3, because:
- Leading term: x2⋅2x2=2x4x^2 \cdot 2x^2 = 2x^4x2⋅2x2=2x4
- Constant term: −4⋅3=−12-4 \cdot 3 = -12−4⋅3=−12

Expanding (x2−4)(2x2+kx+3)(x^2 - 4)(2x^2 + kx + 3)(x2−4)(2x2+kx+3), the x3x^3x3 term comes solely from x2⋅(kx)=kx3x^2 \cdot (kx) = kx^3x2⋅(kx)=kx3. Setting this equal to the x3x^3x3 term of P(x)P(x)P(x), which is −7x3-7x^3−7x3, gives k=−7k = -7k=−7.

Thus, the quotient is:
Q(x)=2x2−7x+3Q(x) = 2x^2 - 7x + 3Q(x)=2x2−7x+3
Factoring Q(x)Q(x)Q(x):
2x2−7x+3=(2x−1)(x−3)2x^2 - 7x + 3 = (2x - 1)(x - 3)2x2−7x+3=(2x−1)(x−3)
Therefore, the complete factorization of P(x)P(x)P(x) is:
P(x)=(x−2)(x+2)(2x−1)(x−3)P(x) = (x - 2)(x + 2)(2x - 1)(x - 3)P(x)=(x−2)(x+2)(2x−1)(x−3)
Among the choices, 2x−12x - 12x−1 is a factor of P(x)P(x)P(x).

Method 2: Factor Theorem
Since x2−4=(x−2)(x+2)x^2 - 4 = (x - 2)(x + 2)x2−4=(x−2)(x+2) divides P(x)P(x)P(x), both x=2x = 2x=2 and x=−2x = -2x=−2 must be roots:
- P(2)=2(16)−7(8)+4a+2b−12=4a+2b−36=0  ⟹  2a+b=18P(2) = 2(16) - 7(8) + 4a + 2b - 12 = 4a + 2b - 36 = 0 \implies 2a + b = 18P(2)=2(16)−7(8)+4a+2b−12=4a+2b−36=0⟹2a+b=18
- P(−2)=2(16)−7(−8)+4a−2b−12=4a−2b+76=0  ⟹  2a−b=−38P(-2) = 2(16) - 7(-8) + 4a - 2b - 12 = 4a - 2b + 76 = 0 \implies 2a - b = -38P(−2)=2(16)−7(−8)+4a−2b−12=4a−2b+76=0⟹2a−b=−38

Adding these two equations yields 4a=−20  ⟹  a=−54a = -20 \implies a = -54a=−20⟹a=−5, which gives b=28b = 28b=28.
Dividing P(x)=2x4−7x3−5x2+28x−12P(x) = 2x^4 - 7x^3 - 5x^2 + 28x - 12P(x)=2x4−7x3−5x2+28x−12 by x2−4x^2 - 4x2−4 yields 2x2−7x+3=(2x−1)(x−3)2x^2 - 7x + 3 = (2x - 1)(x - 3)2x2−7x+3=(2x−1)(x−3).

Common Traps:
- Choice B (2x+12x + 12x+1) and Choice C (x+3x + 3x+3) arise from sign errors when factoring the quadratic 2x2−7x+32x^2 - 7x + 32x2−7x+3.
- Choice D (2x−32x - 32x−3) arises from incorrectly pairing the factors of the constant 333 and leading coefficient 222, such as thinking (2x−3)(x−1)=2x2−5x+3(2x - 3)(x - 1) = 2x^2 - 5x + 3(2x−3)(x−1)=2x2−5x+3.
