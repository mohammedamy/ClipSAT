14 Quadratic Functions Standard [1 mark]
In the figure below, triangle ABCABCABC is a right triangle with right angle at CCC, and altitude CDCDCD is drawn to hypotenuse ABABAB. The length of altitude CDCDCD is 666, AD=xAD = xAD=x, and DB=x+5DB = x + 5DB=x+5. What is the length of hypotenuse ABABAB?
A. 131313
B. 171717
C. 999
D. 444
Correct Answer: (A) — 131313
By the Geometric Mean Theorem (Altitude Rule) for right triangles, the altitude to the hypotenuse is the geometric mean of the two segments of the hypotenuse:

CD2=AD⋅DBCD^2 = AD \cdot DBCD2=AD⋅DB

Substitute the given values into the equation:
62=x(x+5)6^2 = x(x + 5)62=x(x+5)
36=x2+5x36 = x^2 + 5x36=x2+5x

Rearrange into standard quadratic form:
x2+5x−36=0x^2 + 5x - 36 = 0x2+5x−36=0

Factor the quadratic equation:
(x+9)(x−4)=0(x + 9)(x - 4) = 0(x+9)(x−4)=0

This gives two solutions: x=−9x = -9x=−9 and x=4x = 4x=4. Because AD=xAD = xAD=x represents a geometric length, it must be positive. Thus, we reject the extraneous negative root x=−9x = -9x=−9, which leaves x=4x = 4x=4.

Finally, calculate the length of hypotenuse ABABAB:
AB=AD+DB=x+(x+5)=4+(4+5)=4+9=13AB = AD + DB = x + (x + 5) = 4 + (4 + 5) = 4 + 9 = 13AB=AD+DB=x+(x+5)=4+(4+5)=4+9=13.

- Choice B (444) is an incomplete solve trap: finding xxx but forgetting to compute the total hypotenuse ABABAB.
