15 Statistics & Data Analysis Standard [1 mark]
The table below shows the results of a survey on the preferred music genres of students in two different schools, School A and School B. The total number of students surveyed in School A is 200, and the total number of students surveyed in School B is 150.

If a student is chosen at random from School A, what is the probability that they prefer Electronic music?
A. 0.250.250.25
B. 0.150.150.15
C. 0.330.330.33
D. 0.200.200.20
Correct Answer: (D) — 0.200.200.20
The question asks for the probability that a randomly chosen student from School A prefers Electronic music.

First, identify the number of students in School A who prefer Electronic music from the table. This number is 40.

Next, identify the total number of students surveyed in School A. This number is given as 200.

To find the probability, divide the number of students who prefer Electronic music by the total number of students in School A:

P(Electronic | School A) = (Number of students in School A who prefer Electronic) / (Total number of students in School A)

P(Electronic | School A) = 40 / 200

Simplify the fraction:

40 / 200 = 4 / 20 = 1 / 5

Convert the fraction to a decimal:

1 / 5 = 0.20

Therefore, the probability that a randomly chosen student from School A prefers Electronic music is 0.20.

20 Linear Equations & Lines Challenging [1 mark]
Consider a triangle ABC. The line segment AD is the angle bisector of angle BAC, where D lies on BC. Points E and F are on AC and AB respectively, such that EF is parallel to BC. Given that AB = 10 units, AC = 15 units, BD = 6 units, and the area of triangle AEF is 49\frac{4}{9}94​ of the area of triangle ABC. Determine the length of CD in units.
A. 121212
B. 151515
C. 999
D. 7.57.57.5
Correct Answer: (C) — 999
Let CD = x. By the Angle Bisector Theorem, ABAC=BDCD\frac{AB}{AC} = \frac{BD}{CD}ACAB​=CDBD​. Substituting the given values, we have 1015=6x\frac{10}{15} = \frac{6}{x}1510​=x6​. This simplifies to 10x=15×610x = 15 \times 610x=15×6, so 10x=9010x = 9010x=90, which gives x=9x = 9x=9. Thus, CD = 9 units.

Since EF is parallel to BC, triangle AEF is similar to triangle ABC. The ratio of the areas of two similar triangles is the square of the ratio of their corresponding sides. Given that the area of triangle AEF is 49\frac{4}{9}94​ of the area of triangle ABC, we have Area(AEF)Area(ABC)=(AEAC)2=(AFAB)2=49\frac{Area(AEF)}{Area(ABC)} = \left(\frac{AE}{AC}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{AF}{AB}\right)^2 = \frac{4}{9}Area(ABC)Area(AEF)​=(ACAE​)2=(ABAF​)2=94​.

This implies that AEAC=AFAB=49=23\frac{AE}{AC} = \frac{AF}{AB} = \sqrt{\frac{4}{9}} = \frac{2}{3}ACAE​=ABAF​=94​​=32​.

From AEAC=23\frac{AE}{AC} = \frac{2}{3}ACAE​=32​, we get AE=23AC=23×15=10AE = \frac{2}{3} AC = \frac{2}{3} \times 15 = 10AE=32​AC=32​×15=10. Also, from AFAB=23\frac{AF}{AB} = \frac{2}{3}ABAF​=32​, we get AF=23AB=23×10=203AF = \frac{2}{3} AB = \frac{2}{3} \times 10 = \frac{20}{3}AF=32​AB=32​×10=320​.

The problem asks for the length of CD. We have already found CD = 9 using the Angle Bisector Theorem. The information about the areas of the triangles and the similarity of AEF and ABC is consistent with the given side lengths but not necessary for finding CD. However, it serves as a check or an indicator of the problem's synthesis requirement.
