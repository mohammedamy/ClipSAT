What is the fifth term in an arithmetic sequence where the first term is 7 and the common difference is 3?
A. 222222
B. 191919
C. 212121
D. 202020
Correct Answer: (B) — 191919
في المتتابعة الحسابية، يُعطى الحد الـ nnn بالصيغة an=a1+(n−1)da_n = a_1 + (n-1)dan​=a1​+(n−1)d، حيث a1a_1a1​ هو الحد الأول و ddd هو الفرق المشترك. في هذه الحالة، a1=7a_1 = 7a1​=7 و d=3d = 3d=3. لإيجاد الحد الخامس (n=5n = 5n=5)، نعوض في الصيغة:

a5=7+(5−1)×3a_5 = 7 + (5-1) \times 3a5​=7+(5−1)×3

a5=7+(4)×3a_5 = 7 + (4) \times 3a5​=7+(4)×3

a5=7+12a_5 = 7 + 12a5​=7+12

a5=19a_5 = 19a5​=19
---

In an arithmetic sequence, the nnn-th term is given by the formula an=a1+(n−1)da_n = a_1 + (n-1)dan​=a1​+(n−1)d, where a1a_1a1​ is the first term and ddd is the common difference. In this case, a1=7a_1 = 7a1​=7 and d=3d = 3d=3. To find the fifth term (n=5n = 5n=5), we substitute into the formula:

a5=7+(5−1)×3a_5 = 7 + (5-1) \times 3a5​=7+(5−1)×3

a5=7+(4)×3a_5 = 7 + (4) \times 3a5​=7+(4)×3

a5=7+12a_5 = 7 + 12a5​=7+12

a5=19a_5 = 19a5​=19
