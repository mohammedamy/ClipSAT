16 Data Analysis & Probability Foundational [1 mark]
In a study of 1000 university students regarding their coffee consumption habits, it was found that 60% drink coffee daily, and 40% drink it at least once a week. Of those who drink coffee daily, 70% prefer black coffee. Of those who drink coffee at least once a week, 50% prefer black coffee. If a student is randomly selected from this sample, what is the probability that the student prefers black coffee, given that they do not drink coffee daily?
A. 0.500.500.50
B. 0.300.300.30
C. 0.200.200.20
D. 0.400.400.40
Correct Answer: (A) — 0.500.500.50
Let D be the event that a student drinks coffee daily, and W be the event that a student drinks coffee at least once a week. Let B be the event that a student prefers black coffee.

We are given:
P(D)=0.60P(D) = 0.60P(D)=0.60
P(W)=0.40P(W) = 0.40P(W)=0.40
P(B∣D)=0.70P(B|D) = 0.70P(B∣D)=0.70
P(B∣W)=0.50P(B|W) = 0.50P(B∣W)=0.50

We want to find the probability that a student prefers black coffee, given that they do not drink coffee daily. The event 'does not drink coffee daily' is equivalent to 'drinks coffee at least once a week', which is event W.

So we need to find P(B∣W)P(B|W)P(B∣W). This is directly given in the problem statement.

P(B∣W)=0.50P(B|W) = 0.50P(B∣W)=0.50

Therefore, the probability that the student prefers black coffee given that they do not drink coffee daily is 0.50.

بالعربية:

لتكن D حدث أن الطالب يشرب القهوة يوميًا، ولتكن W حدث أن الطالب يشرب القهوة مرة واحدة في الأسبوع على الأقل. ولتكن B حدث أن الطالب يفضل القهوة السوداء.

المعطيات هي:
P(D)=0.60P(D) = 0.60P(D)=0.60
P(W)=0.40P(W) = 0.40P(W)=0.40
P(B∣D)=0.70P(B|D) = 0.70P(B∣D)=0.70
P(B∣W)=0.50P(B|W) = 0.50P(B∣W)=0.50

المطلوب هو إيجاد احتمال أن الطالب يفضل القهوة السوداء، علمًا بأنه لا يشرب القهوة يوميًا. حدث 'لا يشرب القهوة يوميًا' يكافئ حدث 'يشرب القهوة مرة واحدة في الأسبوع على الأقل'، وهو الحدث W.

لذا، نحتاج إلى إيجاد P(B∣W)P(B|W)P(B∣W). هذه القيمة معطاة مباشرة في نص المسألة.

P(B∣W)=0.50P(B|W) = 0.50P(B∣W)=0.50

إذن، احتمال أن يفضل الطالب القهوة السوداء علمًا بأنه لا يشرب القهوة يوميًا هو 0.50.

52 Arithmetic & Number Sense Challenging [1 mark]
We have the equation x2−6x+k=0x^2 - 6x + k = 0x2−6x+k=0, where kkk is a constant. If the sum of the roots of this quadratic equation is 666 and the product of its roots is 555, what is the value of kkk, and what is the larger of the two roots?

لأي معادلة تربيعية بالصيغة ax2+bx+c=0ax^2 + bx + c = 0ax2+bx+c=0، يكون مجموع الجذور هو −b/a-b/a−b/a وحاصل ضرب الجذور هو c/ac/ac/a.

For any quadratic equation in the form ax2+bx+c=0ax^2 + bx + c = 0ax2+bx+c=0, the sum of the roots is −b/a-b/a−b/a and the product of the roots is c/ac/ac/a.
