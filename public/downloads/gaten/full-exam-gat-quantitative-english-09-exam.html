16 Motion, Speed & Work Rates Foundational [1 mark]
In a workshop, a painter paints cars. The painter charges a fee of 50 SAR for each car they complete, plus a flat fee of 200 SAR for setup at the start of each day. If the total amount earned by the painter in one day was 750 SAR, what is the number of cars the painter completed that day?
A. 4 cars
B. 5 cars
C. 11 cars
D. 7 cars
Correct Answer: (C) — 11 cars
To solve this problem, we need to find the number of cars the painter completed. Let 'x' represent the number of cars painted.
The total amount earned by the painter consists of the fixed setup fee and the fee for the cars painted.
Fee for cars painted = (Price per car) × (Number of cars)
Total Earnings = Setup Fee + (Price per car × Number of cars)
We are given: Total Earnings = 750 SAR, Setup Fee = 200 SAR, Price per car = 50 SAR.
So, we can write the following equation:
750 = 200 + (50 × x)
Now, we solve the equation for 'x':
Subtract 200 from both sides:
750 - 200 = 50 × x
550 = 50 × x
Divide both sides by 50:
x = 550 / 50
x = 11
Therefore, the painter completed 11 cars.
