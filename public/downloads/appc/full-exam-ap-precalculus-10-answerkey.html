A right-angled triangle ABC is shown with the right angle at vertex C. The length of side AC is 8 units and the length of side BC is 6 units. What is the value of sin⁡(∠BAC)\sin(\angle BAC)sin(∠BAC)?
A. 43\frac{4}{3}34​
B. 35\frac{3}{5}53​ ✓
C. 45\frac{4}{5}54​
D. 34\frac{3}{4}43​
Correct Answer: (B) — 35\frac{3}{5}53​
The triangle ABC is a right-angled triangle with the right angle at C. We are given AC = 8 and BC = 6. We want to find sin⁡(∠BAC)\sin(\angle BAC)sin(∠BAC).

In a right-angled triangle, the sine of an angle is defined as the ratio of the length of the side opposite the angle to the length of the hypotenuse.

First, we need to find the length of the hypotenuse AB using the Pythagorean theorem: AB2=AC2+BC2AB^2 = AC^2 + BC^2AB2=AC2+BC2.
AB2=82+62AB^2 = 8^2 + 6^2AB2=82+62
AB2=64+36AB^2 = 64 + 36AB2=64+36
