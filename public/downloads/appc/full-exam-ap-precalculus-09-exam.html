We are given that the x-coordinate of P is 12\frac{1}{2}21​, so cos⁡θ=12\cos \theta = \frac{1}{2}cosθ=21​. Since P is on the unit circle, its coordinates satisfy the equation x2+y2=1x^2 + y^2 = 1x2+y2=1. Substituting the x-coordinate, we get (12)2+y2=1(\frac{1}{2})^2 + y^2 = 1(21​)2+y2=1. Taking the square root of both sides, we get y=±32y = \pm \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}y=±23​​. Since the problem states that θ\thetaθ is in the first quadrant, the y-coordinate must be positive. Therefore, the y-coordinate of P is 32\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}23​​. [M1][A1]
