A survey of 800 randomly selected voters found that 65% support a new public transit initiative, with an associated margin of error of 4 percentage points at a 95% confidence level. Which of the following is the most plausible conclusion from this result?

Correct Answer: (D) — It is plausible that the true proportion of voters in the entire population who support the initiative is between 61% and 69%.
A sample estimate with a margin of error ±m\pm m±m creates a confidence interval [p−m,p+m][p - m, p + m][p−m,p+m] for the true population parameter.
Here, the interval is [65−4%,65+4%]=[61%,69%][65 - 4\%, 65 + 4\%] = [61\%, 69\%][65−4%,65+4%]=[61%,69%].
This interval represents plausible values for the actual percentage of the entire target population, not just the sample.
