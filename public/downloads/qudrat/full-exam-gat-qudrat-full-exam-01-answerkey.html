A bakery has two types of cakes: chocolate and vanilla. The ratio of the number of chocolate cakes to vanilla cakes is 5:35:35:3. If the total number of chocolate and vanilla cakes is 484848 pieces, what is the number of chocolate cakes?
A. 282828
B. 202020
C. 181818
D. 303030 ✓
Correct Answer: (D) — 303030
Let the number of chocolate cakes be 5x5x5x and the number of vanilla cakes be 3x3x3x, based on the given ratio of 5:35:35:3. The total number of cakes is the sum of chocolate and vanilla cakes, which is 5x+3x=485x + 3x = 485x+3x=48. Combining like terms, we get 8x=488x = 488x=48. Dividing both sides by 8, we find x=6x = 6x=6. The number of chocolate cakes is 5x5x5x, so 5×6=305 \times 6 = 305×6=30. The number of vanilla cakes is 3x3x3x, so 3×6=183 \times 6 = 183×6=18. The total is 30+18=4830 + 18 = 4830+18=48. Therefore, the number of chocolate cakes is 30.
