16 Data Analysis & Probability Foundational [1 mark]
In a survey of 100 students about their favorite sports, it was found that 60 students prefer football, 50 students prefer basketball, and 20 students prefer both. What is the percentage of students who prefer neither of these two sports?
A. 2020%20
B. 3030%30
C. 1010%10
D. 4040%40
Correct Answer: (C) — 1010%10
To determine the percentage of students who prefer neither sport, we use the principle of inclusion-exclusion.

Number of students who prefer only football = (Number of students who prefer football) - (Number of students who prefer both)
= 60 - 20 = 40 students.

Number of students who prefer only basketball = (Number of students who prefer basketball) - (Number of students who prefer both)
= 50 - 20 = 30 students.

Total number of students who prefer football OR basketball OR both = (Number who prefer only football) + (Number who prefer only basketball) + (Number who prefer both)
= 40 + 30 + 20 = 90 students.

Number of students who prefer neither sport = (Total number of students) - (Total number of students who prefer football OR basketball OR both)
= 100 - 90 = 10 students.

To calculate the percentage of students who prefer neither sport:
Percentage = (Number of students who prefer neither sport / Total number of students) * 100
= (10 / 100) * 100 = 10%.

A. 181818
B. 161616
C. 222222
D. 202020
Correct Answer: (D) — 202020
To solve this problem, we first need to calculate the total sum of points for all four students. Since the average score is 15 points and there are 4 students, the total sum of points is 15×4=6015 \times 4 = 6015×4=60 points.

From the bar chart, we can see the points scored by the first three students: Student 1 scored 10 points, Student 2 scored 20 points, and Student 3 scored 12 points.

The sum of points for the first three students is 10+20+12=4210 + 20 + 12 = 4210+20+12=42 points.

To find the number of points scored by the fourth student, we subtract the sum of points of the first three students from the total sum of points: 60−42=1860 - 42 = 1860−42=18 points.

Therefore, the number of points scored by the fourth student is 18 points.
